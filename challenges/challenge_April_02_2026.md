DAILY PROFESSIONAL AI CHALLENGE – April 02, 2026

🏢 Business Scenario
You're working with a mid-size consulting firm that's struggling with proposal response times and inconsistent pricing across partners. The firm receives 40-50 RFPs monthly but can only respond to 60% due to resource constraints in proposal development. Partners price engagements based on intuition rather than data, leading to 35% variance in similar project types. The firm needs an intelligent system to quickly assess RFP viability and generate standardized pricing recommendations.

🎯 Objective
Build an automated RFP evaluation and pricing recommendation system that can process incoming RFPs, assess win probability based on firm capabilities, and generate competitive pricing suggestions within 2 hours instead of the current 2-day manual process.

🧩 Technical Requirement
Create a comprehensive document analysis system that extracts key requirements from RFP documents, matches them against firm capabilities, calculates complexity scores, and generates pricing recommendations using historical engagement data.

TOOL FOCUS FOR TODAY:
Mixed Tools (Claude Code + Integration)

📂 Inputs
- Sample RFP documents (assume 12 different RFPs across various industries)
- Historical engagement database with project details, scope, team size, duration, and final pricing
- Firm capability matrix showing service lines, industry expertise, and resource availability
- Competitor pricing intelligence from past wins/losses
- Partner preferences and risk tolerance settings

📈 Business Deliverables
- RFP viability dashboard showing go/no-go recommendations with confidence scores
- Automated pricing calculator with competitive positioning analysis
- Resource requirement estimates for each viable opportunity
- Risk-adjusted win probability assessment for each RFP
- Weekly pipeline report showing prioritized opportunities and resource allocation

🧠 Business Impact
Enables the firm to respond to 85% more RFPs while reducing proposal development time by 70%. Standardizes pricing decisions to reduce variance from 35% to under 15%, potentially increasing margins by 8-12%. Creates data-driven bid/no-bid process that improves win rates and resource allocation efficiency.
