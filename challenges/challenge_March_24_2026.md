DAILY PROFESSIONAL AI CHALLENGE – March 24, 2026

🏢 Business Scenario
You're working with a mid-sized technology company that's struggling with employee retention. The company has 450 employees across engineering, sales, marketing, and operations. Exit interviews reveal inconsistent feedback, HR lacks data-driven insights into resignation patterns, and managers aren't getting early warning signals about at-risk employees. The CHRO wants to move from reactive exit management to predictive retention strategy.

🎯 Objective
Build an intelligent employee retention prediction system that identifies flight risk employees 60-90 days before they resign and generates targeted intervention recommendations to reduce voluntary turnover from 18% to under 12%.

🧩 Technical Requirement
Create a machine learning-based risk scoring system that processes multiple employee data signals (performance, engagement, tenure, compensation, manager feedback) and generates actionable retention strategies with confidence intervals.

TOOL FOCUS FOR TODAY:
Mixed Tools (Claude Code + Integration)

📂 Inputs
- Employee performance review data (last 18 months)
- Survey responses from quarterly engagement surveys
- Compensation and promotion history
- Manager 1:1 meeting frequency and quality ratings
- Team composition and reporting structure changes
- Historical resignation data with exit interview themes
- Industry salary benchmark data

📈 Business Deliverables
- Flight risk dashboard showing top 25 at-risk employees with risk scores
- Manager action plans with specific retention interventions for each at-risk employee
- Compensation gap analysis identifying underpaid high performers
- Departmental retention health scorecard with trend analysis
- ROI calculator showing cost savings from improved retention rates

🧠 Business Impact
Reduces recruitment costs (avg $15K per replacement), preserves institutional knowledge, improves team stability, and enables proactive people management. A 6% reduction in turnover saves approximately $405K annually in direct costs plus immeasurable productivity gains.

🛠 Allowed Tools
- Claude Code for data processing and risk modeling
- Spreadsheet tools for data integration and dashboard creation
- Simple database (SQLite/Supabase) for employee data management
- Email automation tools for manager notifications
- Basic web interface for HR dashboard access

⏱ Time Box
Target: 90-120 minutes

📁 Portfolio Artefact
Save the complete retention prediction system including risk model, manager intervention playbooks, and executive dashboard. This demonstrates advanced people analytics capabilities highly valued in HR tech and consulting roles.

🎓 Skills Trained
- People analytics and predictive HR modeling
- Multi-factor risk assessment and scoring algorithms
- Change management and intervention strategy design
- HR technology and employee lifecycle optimization
- Strategic workforce planning and retention economics

🧪 Success Criteria
1. ✅ Risk model accurately identifies employees with 70%+ flight probability
2. ✅ Manager intervention recommendations are specific and actionable (not generic)
3. ✅ Cost-benefit analysis shows clear ROI from retention improvements
4. ✅ Dashboard provides both individual risk scores and departmental trend insights
5. ✅ System generates automated alerts for newly at-risk employees
