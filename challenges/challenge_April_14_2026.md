DAILY PROFESSIONAL AI CHALLENGE – April 14, 2026

🏢 Business Scenario
You're the operations consultant for a mid-sized law firm handling 300+ active cases across litigation, corporate, and family law. Partners are concerned about declining profitability despite increased caseloads. Case files contain inconsistent data, billing inefficiencies are widespread, and attorneys struggle to prioritize work effectively. The managing partner needs actionable insights to optimize case management and improve firm-wide profitability within 90 days.

🎯 Objective
Build an intelligent legal case portfolio optimization system that analyzes case data to identify profitability patterns, predict resource needs, and generate strategic recommendations for improving case selection and resource allocation.

🧩 Technical Requirement
Create a comprehensive case analytics engine that processes mixed legal data formats, performs profitability modeling, and generates predictive insights for strategic case management decisions.

TOOL FOCUS FOR TODAY:
Primarily Claude Code

📂 Inputs
- Case database with 18 months of matters (case type, duration, billing rates, outcomes)
- Attorney utilization data with billing hours and realization rates
- Client information including payment histories and matter values
- Historical settlement/judgment data by case type and attorney

📈 Business Deliverables
1. Case profitability analysis identifying high/low-value matter types
2. Attorney productivity optimization recommendations by practice area
3. Case duration prediction model for better resource planning
4. Client portfolio analysis with retention and growth opportunities
5. Strategic case selection framework for new matter evaluation

🧠 Business Impact
Enables data-driven case management decisions that could improve firm profitability by 15-25%. Provides objective criteria for case acceptance/rejection, optimizes attorney assignments, and identifies training needs. Creates systematic approach to practice area growth and client relationship management.

🛠 Allowed Tools
- Claude Code (primary)
- Spreadsheets for data organization
- Simple visualization tools

⏱ Time Box
Target: 90-120 minutes

📁 Portfolio Artefact
Legal practice optimization dashboard with predictive case management recommendations and ROI projections

🎓 Skills Trained
- Legal industry business intelligence
- Multi-practice profitability analysis
- Predictive resource planning
- Professional services optimization
- Strategic business analysis

🧪 Success Criteria
1. ✅ Case profitability model ranks matter types by average margin and identifies top 5 most/least profitable practice areas
2. ✅ Attorney performance analysis shows productivity scores and optimal case-attorney matching recommendations
3. ✅ Case duration prediction model estimates timeline for new matters with 75%+ accuracy
4. ✅ Client analysis identifies top 15 growth opportunities and 10 at-risk relationships
5. ✅ Strategic framework provides clear go/no-go criteria for new case evaluation
