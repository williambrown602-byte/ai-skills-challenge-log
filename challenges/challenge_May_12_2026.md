DAILY PROFESSIONAL AI CHALLENGE – May 12, 2026

🏢 Business Scenario
You're the Chief Analytics Officer at a fast-growing B2B software company that sells to mid-market enterprises. The company has grown from $12M to $85M ARR in 18 months but is struggling with declining gross margins (dropped from 78% to 61%) and increasing customer acquisition costs. The CEO needs to understand what's driving these concerning trends and wants actionable recommendations for the quarterly board meeting in 3 days. The finance team has provided fragmented cost data, but there's no unified view of unit economics across customer segments, products, or acquisition channels.

🎯 Objective
Build an intelligent unit economics analysis engine that consolidates cost and revenue data to identify margin erosion drivers, segment profitability patterns, and generate strategic optimization recommendations to restore gross margins above 70% within 6 months.

🧩 Technical Requirement
Develop a comprehensive business intelligence system that performs multi-dimensional profitability analysis, identifies statistical correlations between operational metrics and margin performance, and creates predictive models for unit economics optimization.

TOOL FOCUS FOR TODAY:
Primarily Claude Code

📂 Inputs
- 18 months of customer revenue data by segment (Enterprise, Mid-Market, SMB)
- Cost of Goods Sold breakdown (hosting, support, integrations, professional services)
- Customer acquisition data by channel (Sales, Marketing, Partnerships)
- Product usage metrics and feature adoption rates
- Support ticket volume and resolution costs by customer tier
- Professional services delivery hours and associated costs

📈 Business Deliverables
- Unit economics dashboard showing profitability by customer segment and acquisition channel
- Margin erosion root cause analysis with quantified impact of each driver
- Customer lifetime value optimization recommendations with projected ROI
- Strategic action plan to achieve 70%+ gross margin target within 6 months
- Board-ready executive presentation with 3 key strategic recommendations

🧠 Business Impact
Transform fragmented financial data into strategic intelligence that guides critical business decisions. Enable executives to identify which customer segments and products drive profitable growth versus those that drain resources. Provide data-driven roadmap for margin recovery that could improve company valuation by $50-100M ahead of Series C fundraising.

🛠 Allowed Tools
- Claude Code for data analysis, statistical modeling, and report generation
- Spreadsheet software for data input and validation
- Basic visualization tools for dashboard creation

⏱ Time Box
Target: 90-120 minutes

📁 Portfolio Artefact
Unit economics intelligence system with executive dashboard, statistical analysis models, and board-ready strategic recommendations that demonstrate advanced business analytics capabilities for senior finance/strategy roles.

🎓 Skills Trained

- Statistical correlation analysis for business intelligence
- Executive-level financial communication and board reporting
- Strategic business planning with quantified impact projections

3. ✅ Optimization recommendations include specific actions, timelines, and projected financial impact
