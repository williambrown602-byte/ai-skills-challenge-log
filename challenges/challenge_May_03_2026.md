DAILY PROFESSIONAL AI CHALLENGE – May 03, 2026

🏢 Business Scenario
You're the Director of Strategy at a mid-sized management consulting firm experiencing rapid growth but struggling with knowledge management. Critical insights from completed engagements are scattered across presentations, emails, and individual consultant expertise. The firm loses competitive advantage when top consultants leave, and junior staff repeatedly solve problems that were addressed months earlier. Partners need a systematic way to capture, organize, and leverage intellectual capital to improve proposal quality and accelerate project delivery.

🎯 Objective
Create an intelligent knowledge extraction and expertise mapping system that transforms historical project documents into a searchable strategic asset, identifies subject matter experts, and generates knowledge-based recommendations for current engagements.

🧩 Technical Requirement
Build a document processing pipeline that extracts insights, themes, and methodologies from project archives, creates expertise profiles, and develops a recommendation engine for knowledge reuse.

TOOL FOCUS FOR TODAY:
Primarily Claude Code

📂 Inputs
- 180 completed project documents (proposals, deliverables, lessons learned)
- Consultant assignment history showing who worked on which projects
- Current active project descriptions requiring knowledge support
- Industry classification data for all historical engagements

📈 Business Deliverables
- Strategic knowledge repository with searchable insights and methodologies
- Consultant expertise mapping showing subject matter specializations
- Knowledge gap analysis identifying areas lacking internal expertise
- Active project support recommendations linking current needs to historical solutions
- Partner presentation quantifying knowledge management ROI potential

🧠 Business Impact
Reduces proposal development time by 40%, accelerates junior consultant learning curve, prevents knowledge loss during turnover, and creates competitive advantage through systematized intellectual capital. Enables data-driven staffing decisions and identifies external expertise needs.

🛠 Allowed Tools
Claude Code, Python libraries for text processing, basic visualization tools, spreadsheet integration for data organization

⏱ Time Box
Target: 90-120 minutes

📁 Portfolio Artefact
Complete knowledge management system with extraction pipeline, expertise database, and recommendation engine that demonstrates advanced document processing and business intelligence capabilities.

🎓 Skills Trained
- Advanced document processing and text analysis
- Knowledge management system architecture
- Expertise mapping and organizational intelligence
- Strategic business analysis and recommendations
- Professional services industry optimization

🧪 Success Criteria
1. Successfully processes and categorizes 180+ project documents by theme and methodology
2. Creates accurate expertise profiles for consultants based on project history
3. Generates relevant knowledge recommendations for 5+ active projects
4. Identifies top 10 knowledge gaps requiring external expertise or training
5. Quantifies potential time savings and competitive advantages from systematic knowledge reuse

🚀 Stretch Goals
- Implement similarity scoring to find analogous past projects for current challenges
- Create automated knowledge capture templates for ongoing projects
- Build expertise succession planning recommendations identifying knowledge transfer priorities
- Generate strategic hiring recommendations based on knowledge gap analysis
