DAILY PROFESSIONAL AI CHALLENGE – April 27, 2026

🏢 Business Scenario
You're the analytics lead at a mid-sized accounting firm during the post-tax season period. Partners are concerned about client retention as several competitors are aggressively poaching clients with lower fees. The firm needs to identify which clients are at highest risk of leaving and develop targeted retention strategies before the summer planning period. Historical data shows 15% annual client turnover, but early indicators suggest this could spike to 22% without intervention.

🎯 Objective
Build an intelligent client retention prediction system that identifies at-risk clients 60-90 days before they defect and generates personalized retention strategies with ROI projections to reduce client churn from projected 22% back to 12%.

🧩 Technical Requirement
Create a multi-factor client risk assessment model that processes client engagement data, billing patterns, and relationship indicators to generate retention probability scores and automated intervention recommendations.

TOOL FOCUS FOR TODAY:
Primarily Claude Code

📂 Inputs
- Client portfolio data (450 clients across 5 service lines)
- 3-year billing history with service mix breakdown
- Engagement metrics (response times, meeting frequency, partner interactions)
- Competitive intelligence on pricing pressure points
- Historical churn data with exit reasons and client characteristics

📈 Business Deliverables
- Top 75 at-risk clients ranked by churn probability with specific risk factors
- Personalized retention playbooks for each risk category (pricing, service, relationship)
- Revenue protection analysis showing potential losses and intervention costs
- Partner action plans with prioritized client outreach strategies
- Competitive positioning analysis with pricing adjustment recommendations

🧠 Business Impact
Enables proactive client relationship management, protects $2.3M in annual recurring revenue, reduces partner firefighting time by 60%, and provides data-driven competitive positioning strategies for sustainable client retention.

🛠 Allowed Tools

- Spreadsheet software for data validation
