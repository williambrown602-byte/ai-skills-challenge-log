DAILY PROFESSIONAL AI CHALLENGE – March 31, 2026

🏢 Business Scenario
You're the financial analyst at a rapidly growing SaaS company with $50M ARR. The CFO needs to prepare for the quarterly board meeting tomorrow and requires a comprehensive financial health analysis system that can automatically identify trends, risks, and opportunities across all key SaaS metrics. The current manual process takes 2 full days and often misses critical insights that could impact strategic decisions.

🎯 Objective
Build an intelligent SaaS financial health monitoring system that automatically analyzes key metrics, identifies concerning trends, predicts future performance, and generates executive-ready insights for strategic decision-making.

🧩 Technical Requirement
Develop a comprehensive analytics engine that processes multi-dimensional SaaS financial data, applies statistical analysis to identify patterns and anomalies, creates predictive models for key metrics, and generates automated executive summaries with strategic recommendations.

TOOL FOCUS FOR TODAY:
Primarily Claude Code

📂 Inputs
- Monthly recurring revenue data by customer segment (36 months)
- Customer acquisition cost and lifetime value metrics by channel
- Churn rates by customer size and contract type
- Cash flow statements and burn rate data
- Sales pipeline and conversion metrics
- Unit economics by product line

📈 Business Deliverables
- Executive financial dashboard with traffic light indicators for all key SaaS metrics
- Automated trend analysis identifying 5 biggest opportunities and 5 highest risks
- Revenue forecasting model for next 4 quarters with confidence intervals
- Unit economics optimization recommendations with projected impact
- Board-ready executive summary with strategic recommendations and supporting data

🧠 Business Impact
Transform a 2-day manual analysis into a 30-minute automated process, ensure no critical insights are missed, enable data-driven board discussions, and provide predictive capabilities for strategic planning. This system becomes essential for ongoing financial monitoring and investor relations.

🛠 Allowed Tools
- Claude Code (primary)
- Spreadsheet tools for data visualization
- Basic charting libraries if needed

⏱ Time Box
Target: 90-120 minutes

📁 Portfolio Artefact
Complete SaaS financial analysis system with sample analysis, demonstrating advanced business analytics skills and SaaS industry expertise for senior analyst roles.

🎓 Skills Trained
- SaaS metrics analysis and interpretation
- Predictive financial modeling
- Executive-level business communication
- Automated business intelligence system design
- Strategic financial analysis and recommendations

🧪 Success Criteria
1. System correctly calculates and analyzes all standard SaaS metrics (MRR growth, CAC payback, LTV/CAC ratio, etc.)
2. Trend analysis identifies at least 5 actionable insights with supporting statistical evidence
3. Revenue forecasting model provides quarterly projections with realistic confidence intervals
4. Executive summary is board-ready with clear strategic recommendations
5. System processes complex multi-dimensional data and presents insights in accessible format

🚀 Stretch Goals
- Add scenario analysis for different growth strategies
- Include competitive benchmarking against industry standards
- Create automated alert system for metric threshold breaches
- Build investor presentation template with key charts
