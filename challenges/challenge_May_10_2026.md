DAILY PROFESSIONAL AI CHALLENGE – May 10, 2026

🏢 Business Scenario
A fast-growing e-commerce company with 12 warehouses across North America is struggling with inventory allocation decisions. Products are frequently overstocked in some regions while simultaneously out-of-stock in others, causing $2.8M in monthly lost revenue. Regional demand patterns shift seasonally, customer preferences vary by geography, and current manual allocation processes take 3-4 days to execute. The operations team needs an intelligent system to optimize inventory distribution and predict regional demand patterns.

🎯 Objective
Build an intelligent inventory allocation optimization system that analyzes regional sales patterns, predicts demand fluctuations, and generates automated distribution recommendations to reduce stockouts by 45% while minimizing overstock carrying costs.

🧩 Technical Requirement
Create a multi-regional demand prediction model that processes historical sales data, seasonal trends, and regional customer behavior patterns to generate optimal inventory allocation recommendations with confidence intervals.

TOOL FOCUS FOR TODAY:
Mixed Tools (Claude Code + Integration)

📂 Inputs
- 18 months of historical sales data by product/region
- Current inventory levels across all 12 warehouse locations
- Regional customer demographics and purchasing patterns
- Seasonal trend data and promotional calendar
- Supplier lead times and shipping cost matrices
- Product lifecycle stages and margin data

📈 Business Deliverables
- Regional demand forecasting model with 85%+ accuracy for next 90 days
- Automated inventory reallocation recommendations prioritized by revenue impact
- Stockout risk assessment dashboard showing high-priority transfer needs
- Cost-benefit analysis of recommended inventory moves including shipping costs
- Weekly inventory health report with regional performance metrics

🧠 Business Impact
This system transforms reactive inventory management into proactive optimization, directly improving revenue while reducing carrying costs. Demonstrates advanced operations research capabilities, multi-regional analytics expertise, and ability to build systems that impact P&L performance—exactly what senior operations and supply chain roles require.
