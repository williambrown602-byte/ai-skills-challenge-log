DAILY PROFESSIONAL AI CHALLENGE – March 20, 2026

🏢 Business Scenario
You're the new Head of People Analytics at a fast-growing tech startup (280 employees, Series B). The CEO is concerned about high performance variation across teams and wants to understand what drives high-performing teams versus struggling ones. With quarterly reviews coming up and budget planning for next year's headcount, leadership needs data-driven insights to guide team restructuring, manager training investments, and hiring strategy.

🎯 Objective
Build an intelligent team performance analysis system that identifies the key drivers of high-performing teams and generates actionable recommendations for optimizing team composition, management effectiveness, and organizational structure.

🧩 Technical Requirement
Create a comprehensive team analytics engine that processes multi-dimensional performance data, identifies patterns across high/low performing teams, and generates strategic recommendations with confidence scoring.

TOOL FOCUS FOR TODAY:
Mixed Tools (Claude Code + Integration)

📂 Inputs
- Team performance metrics (productivity, goal achievement, cycle time)
- Employee satisfaction survey data (engagement, collaboration, leadership scores)
- Team composition data (seniority mix, skill diversity, team size)
- Manager effectiveness ratings and span of control
- Project delivery metrics by team
- Retention and promotion rates by team

📈 Business Deliverables
- Team Performance Diagnostic Report identifying top/bottom performing teams with root cause analysis
- High-Performance Team Blueprint showing optimal team characteristics
- Manager Development Priority Matrix with targeted training recommendations
- Organizational Restructuring Recommendations for underperforming teams
- ROI projections for implementing team optimization strategies

🧠 Business Impact
Enable leadership to make data-driven decisions about team structure, reduce performance variation by 40%, improve manager effectiveness, and optimize hiring strategy. This directly impacts company productivity, employee satisfaction, and reduces costly team restructuring delays.

🛠 Allowed Tools
- Claude Code (primary analysis engine)
- Spreadsheets for data modeling
- Simple visualization tools
- Basic dashboard creation
- Statistical analysis libraries

⏱ Time Box
Target: 90-120 minutes

📁 Portfolio Artefact
Complete team performance analytics system with executive presentation showing methodology, insights, and strategic recommendations - demonstrates advanced people analytics skills highly valued by growing companies.

🎓 Skills Trained
- People analytics and organizational psychology
- Multi-variate statistical analysis for team dynamics
- Strategic HR recommendations and business impact modeling
- Data-driven leadership development and team optimization
- Executive communication for sensitive organizational topics

🧪 Success Criteria
1. Team performance scoring model accurately ranks teams with clear methodology
2. Statistical analysis identifies 3-5 key drivers of high performance with confidence levels
3. Manager development recommendations are specific and actionable with priority scoring
4. Organizational restructuring suggestions include implementation timeline and risk assessment
5. ROI projections show measurable business impact within 6-12 months

🚀 Stretch Goals
- Create predictive model for new team success probability
- Build automated team composition optimizer for new project staffing
- Generate personalized manager coaching recommendations
- Design team health monitoring dashboard with early warning indicators
