# DAILY PROFESSIONAL AI CHALLENGE – May 28, 2026

🏢 **Business Scenario**
You're the data analytics lead at a mid-sized technology consulting firm that has struggled with project scope creep and margin erosion over the past 18 months. The managing partners have noticed that while the firm wins projects at healthy margins, actual delivered margins are consistently 12-18% below projections. With quarterly partner distributions at risk, leadership needs immediate insights into what's driving scope expansion and how to predict it before it impacts profitability. You have access to 2 years of project data including original scopes, change orders, communication patterns, and final financial outcomes across 180+ engagements.

🎯 **Objective**
Build an intelligent scope change prediction and financial impact system that identifies early warning signals of scope creep, quantifies the financial risk, and generates proactive intervention strategies to protect project margins.

🧩 **Technical Requirement**
Develop a multi-factor predictive model that analyzes project characteristics, client behavior patterns, and team dynamics to predict scope change probability and financial impact with 80%+ accuracy.

**TOOL FOCUS FOR TODAY:** Primarily Claude Code

📂 **Inputs**
- Historical project data (180 projects, 24 months)
- Original scope documents and budgets
- Change order history with approval timelines
- Client communication frequency and sentiment
- Project team composition and seniority levels
- Final project financials and margin calculations
- Industry sector and project type classifications

📈 **Business Deliverables**
- Top 25 active projects ranked by scope creep risk with specific warning indicators
- Scope change probability model for all current engagements
- Financial impact calculator showing potential margin erosion scenarios
- Client risk assessment identifying high-maintenance accounts
- Partner intervention playbook with timing recommendations for at-risk projects

🧠 **Business Impact**
This system protects firm profitability by enabling proactive scope management, reduces partner firefighting time, improves client relationships through better expectation management, and provides data-driven insights for future project pricing and team assignments.

🛠 **Allowed Tools**
- Claude Code (primary)
- Spreadsheet analysis
- Statistical modeling libraries
- Data visualization tools
- Basic automation scripts

⏱ **Time Box**
Target: 90-120 minutes

📁 **Portfolio Artefact**
Comprehensive scope risk management system with predictive dashboard, intervention recommendations, and executive presentation demonstrating potential margin protection value.

🎓 **Skills Trained**
- Predictive risk modeling for professional services
- Multi-factor business analytics and pattern recognition
- Consulting industry financial intelligence and margin protection
- Executive-level risk communication and intervention strategy development
- Statistical analysis for operational decision support

🧪 **Success Criteria**
1. Scope change prediction model achieves 80%+ accuracy on historical validation data
2. Risk scoring system correctly identifies top 10% highest-risk projects from historical dataset
3. Financial impact calculator produces margin erosion estimates within 5% of actual outcomes
4. Intervention timing recommendations are actionable and specific to project phases
5. Executive summary quantifies potential annual margin improvement from proactive management

🚀 **Stretch Goals**
- Develop client relationship health scoring based on scope change patterns
- Create automated alert system for real-time project risk monitoring
- Build ROI calculator showing cost-benefit of dedicated scope management resources
- Generate industry benchmarking analysis for competitive positioning
- Design project team optimization recommendations to minimize scope risk exposure
