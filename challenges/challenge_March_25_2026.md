DAILY PROFESSIONAL AI CHALLENGE – March 25, 2026

🏢 Business Scenario
You're the business analyst at a mid-sized consulting firm where partners are struggling with resource allocation decisions. Currently, 40% of projects experience budget overruns due to poor initial staffing decisions, and senior consultants are either over-allocated (leading to burnout) or under-utilized (reducing profitability). Partners spend 8+ hours weekly in resource planning meetings that often result in suboptimal assignments based on gut feel rather than data.

🎯 Objective
Build an intelligent resource allocation optimization system that analyzes consultant skills, availability, project requirements, and historical performance to generate optimal team compositions and predict project success probability. Reduce resource planning time by 70% while improving project margin predictability.

🧩 Technical Requirement
Create a multi-constraint optimization engine that processes consultant profiles, project parameters, and historical engagement data to generate ranked team composition recommendations with confidence scoring and risk assessment.

TOOL FOCUS FOR TODAY:
Primarily Claude Code

📂 Inputs
- Consultant database (50 consultants with skills, seniority levels, hourly rates, availability)
- Historical project data (200+ past engagements with team compositions, outcomes, margins)
- Current open projects (8 active opportunities requiring staffing decisions)
- Skills taxonomy and project requirement mappings
- Consultant performance ratings and client feedback scores

📈 Business Deliverables
- Optimal team recommendations for all open projects with success probability scores
- Resource utilization optimization showing improved consultant allocation
- Risk-adjusted margin predictions for each proposed team composition
- Consultant development recommendations identifying skill gaps and growth opportunities
- Executive summary quantifying potential improvement in project outcomes

🧠 Business Impact
Partners can make staffing decisions in 30 minutes instead of hours, with data-driven confidence in team success. Improved resource allocation increases billable utilization by 12-15% while reducing project risk. The system creates a competitive advantage through consistently superior team compositions and predictable project outcomes.

🛠 Allowed Tools
- Claude Code (primary environment)
- Python libraries for optimization (scipy, pandas, numpy)
- Data visualization capabilities
- Statistical analysis tools

Target: 90-120 minutes

📁 Portfolio Artefact
Resource allocation optimization system with executive dashboard, team recommendation engine, and business impact analysis - demonstrable expertise in business operations and optimization analytics.

🎓 Skills Trained

- Resource planning and capacity management analytics
- Consulting industry business intelligence
- Predictive modeling for project success
- Executive decision support system design
