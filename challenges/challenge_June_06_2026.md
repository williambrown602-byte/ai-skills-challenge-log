DAILY PROFESSIONAL AI CHALLENGE – June 06, 2026

🏢 Business Scenario
You're the operations analyst for a regional medical device distributor serving 180+ healthcare facilities across 6 states. The company is struggling with inventory inefficiencies—critical surgical supplies are frequently out of stock while non-essential items accumulate excess inventory worth $2.8M. With healthcare facilities demanding 98%+ availability for critical supplies and the company losing 12% of orders to competitors due to stockouts, executive leadership needs a data-driven solution to optimize inventory allocation across 8 distribution centers.

🎯 Objective
Transform scattered inventory and sales data into an intelligent demand forecasting and inventory optimization system that predicts facility-specific demand patterns, identifies optimal safety stock levels, and generates automated reorder recommendations to achieve 98%+ critical supply availability while reducing excess inventory by 60%.

🧩 Technical Requirement
Build a multi-facility demand prediction engine that processes historical consumption patterns, seasonal variations, and facility characteristics to generate facility-specific inventory recommendations with confidence intervals and risk assessments.

TOOL FOCUS FOR TODAY:
Mixed Tools (Claude Code + Integration)

📂 Inputs
- 24 months of facility order history (180 healthcare facilities)
- Current inventory levels across 8 distribution centers
- Product criticality classifications (Critical/Standard/Non-Essential)
- Facility characteristics (bed count, specialty services, patient volume)
- Supplier lead times and minimum order quantities
- Historical stockout incidents and competitor losses

📈 Business Deliverables
- Facility-specific demand forecasting model with 90-day predictions
- Optimal safety stock recommendations by product/facility combination
- Automated reorder point calculator with economic order quantity optimization
- Top 50 inventory reallocation opportunities ranked by financial impact
- Critical supply availability dashboard with real-time stockout risk alerts

🧠 Business Impact
Enable data-driven inventory management that protects critical healthcare operations while optimizing working capital. The system prevents revenue loss from stockouts, reduces carrying costs of excess inventory, and provides competitive advantage through superior supply reliability. This creates measurable value through improved customer satisfaction, reduced emergency shipping costs, and optimized cash flow management.
