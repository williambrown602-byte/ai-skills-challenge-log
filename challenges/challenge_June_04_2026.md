🏢 Business Scenario
You're a data analyst at a mid-sized management consulting firm that's struggling with consultant burnout and declining engagement quality. Recent employee surveys show 68% report being overworked, client satisfaction has dropped from 87% to 76%, and the firm is losing 2-3 senior consultants monthly. Partners need to understand what's driving these issues and how to optimize consultant wellbeing while maintaining project quality and profitability.

🎯 Objective
Build an intelligent consultant workload and wellbeing analytics system that identifies burnout patterns, predicts performance risks, and generates strategic workforce optimization recommendations to restore consultant satisfaction above 80% while maintaining project quality standards.

🧩 Technical Requirement
Create a comprehensive consultant performance and workload analysis engine that processes historical engagement data, performance metrics, and workload patterns to predict consultant health risks and optimize resource allocation across active projects.

TOOL FOCUS FOR TODAY:
Primarily Claude Code
