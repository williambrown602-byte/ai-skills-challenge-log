DAILY PROFESSIONAL AI CHALLENGE – April 09, 2026

🏢 Business Scenario
You're a business analyst at a mid-sized telecommunications company facing increasing customer service costs and declining satisfaction scores. The company receives 15,000+ support tickets monthly across multiple channels (email, chat, phone, social media), with average resolution times of 3.2 days and customer satisfaction scores dropping to 6.8/10. Management needs to understand the root causes of service inefficiency and implement data-driven improvements to reduce costs while improving customer experience.

🎯 Objective
Build an intelligent customer service optimization system that analyzes support ticket patterns, identifies efficiency bottlenecks, predicts resolution times, and generates actionable recommendations to improve service delivery performance and customer satisfaction.

🧩 Technical Requirement
Develop a comprehensive ticket analysis engine that processes multi-channel support data, performs sentiment analysis on customer communications, categorizes issues using pattern recognition, and builds predictive models for resolution time optimization.

TOOL FOCUS FOR TODAY:
Mixed Tools (Claude Code + Integration)

📂 Inputs
- 6 months of support ticket data (ticket ID, channel, category, priority, agent, resolution time, customer feedback)
- Customer communication transcripts and chat logs
- Agent performance metrics and workload data
- Service level agreement (SLA) targets by ticket type
- Customer satisfaction survey responses

📈 Business Deliverables
- Support channel efficiency analysis showing cost per resolution by channel
- Agent workload optimization recommendations with skill-based routing suggestions
- Ticket categorization system with automated priority scoring
- Resolution time prediction model for incoming tickets
- Customer satisfaction improvement action plan with projected ROI

🧠 Business Impact
This system enables the telecommunications company to transform reactive customer service into a proactive, data-driven operation. By optimizing agent allocation, improving first-call resolution rates, and predicting resolution times, the company can reduce service costs by 25-30% while improving customer satisfaction scores to 8.5+/10, directly impacting customer retention and operational efficiency.

🛠 Allowed Tools
- Claude for data analysis, pattern recognition, and recommendation generation
- Spreadsheet software for data processing and dashboard creation
- Basic visualization tools for creating management reports
- Simple automation tools for workflow optimization

Customer Service Optimization Dashboard with ticket analysis insights, agent performance recommendations, and ROI projections that demonstrates advanced analytics capabilities and business impact measurement for telecommunications industry roles.
