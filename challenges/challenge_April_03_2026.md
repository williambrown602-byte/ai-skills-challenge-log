DAILY PROFESSIONAL AI CHALLENGE – April 03, 2026

🏢 Business Scenario
You're working with a mid-size technology company that's struggling with their vendor management process. They work with 180+ vendors across software subscriptions, professional services, and hardware suppliers. The procurement team spends 15 hours per week manually tracking contract renewals, comparing vendor performance, and identifying cost optimization opportunities. Three critical contracts worth $300K were accidentally auto-renewed last quarter because renewal dates were missed. The CFO wants a comprehensive vendor intelligence system to reduce administrative overhead by 60% while preventing costly oversights.

🎯 Objective
Build an intelligent vendor relationship management system that automates contract tracking, evaluates vendor performance against KPIs, identifies consolidation opportunities, and generates strategic procurement recommendations with measurable ROI impact.

🧩 Technical Requirement
Create a comprehensive vendor analytics engine that processes multi-dimensional vendor data to generate automated insights, risk assessments, and optimization recommendations using advanced data modeling and business intelligence techniques.

TOOL FOCUS FOR TODAY:
Mixed Tools (Claude Code + Integration)

📈 Business Deliverables

- Strategic procurement plan with 12-month optimization timeline and projected savings

Transforms reactive vendor management into proactive strategic procurement that prevents costly auto-renewals, identifies underperforming suppliers before they impact operations, and systematically reduces vendor costs through data-driven consolidation. Saves 12+ hours weekly of manual tracking while improving vendor relationship quality and contract terms.
