DAILY PROFESSIONAL AI CHALLENGE – April 17, 2026

🏢 Business Scenario
You're the data analyst for a regional healthcare network managing 8 hospitals and 25 clinics. The Chief Medical Officer is concerned about patient readmission rates, which have increased from 12% to 18% over the past 18 months, costing the network $2.3M annually in penalties and reduced reimbursements. Leadership needs a comprehensive analysis system to identify readmission risk factors and generate evidence-based intervention protocols before the upcoming quality review with Medicare.

🎯 Objective
Build an intelligent patient readmission risk assessment system that analyzes patient data to predict 30-day readmission probability, identifies key clinical and operational risk factors, and generates personalized care transition protocols to reduce readmission rates to below 10%.

🧩 Technical Requirement
Develop a multi-factor risk scoring algorithm that processes patient demographics, medical history, discharge conditions, and social determinants to generate readmission probability scores with clinical decision support recommendations.

TOOL FOCUS FOR TODAY:
Primarily Claude Code

📂 Inputs
Assume access to 18 months of patient discharge data including: patient demographics, primary/secondary diagnoses, length of stay, discharge disposition, insurance type, medication adherence scores, social risk indicators, and 30-day readmission outcomes across all network facilities.

📈 Business Deliverables
- Risk-stratified patient list for next 30 days with intervention priorities
- Clinical protocol recommendations for high-risk patient populations
- Facility-specific readmission pattern analysis with improvement opportunities
- Care coordination workflow recommendations
- Executive dashboard showing projected readmission reduction and cost savings

🧠 Business Impact
Demonstrates ability to apply predictive analytics to clinical operations, generate actionable insights for healthcare quality improvement, and translate complex medical data into strategic business recommendations that directly impact patient outcomes and financial performance.

🛠 Allowed Tools
Claude Code (primary), basic data visualization, statistical analysis functions, healthcare analytics frameworks

⏱ Time Box
Target: 90-120 minutes

📁 Portfolio Artefact
Healthcare analytics system demonstrating clinical risk modeling, quality improvement recommendations, and ability to work with regulated healthcare data while generating measurable business impact.

🎓 Skills Trained
- Healthcare industry analytics and clinical decision support
- Multi-factor risk prediction modeling with confidence intervals
- Healthcare quality improvement methodology
- Regulatory compliance and patient outcome optimization
- Strategic healthcare operations analysis

🧪 Success Criteria
1. Risk scoring model identifies high-risk patients with 75%+ accuracy
2. Clinical intervention protocols are evidence-based and actionable
3. Facility-specific analysis reveals meaningful operational patterns
4. Cost-benefit analysis shows clear ROI projections
5. Dashboard provides real-time decision support for care teams

🚀 Stretch Goals
- Develop medication adherence prediction sub-model
- Create automated care transition checklist generator
- Build comparative facility performance benchmarking
- Generate patient education material recommendations
- Design quality metrics tracking for intervention effectiveness
