DAILY PROFESSIONAL AI CHALLENGE – April 04, 2026

🏢 Business Scenario
You're a business analyst at a fast-growing B2B software company that's struggling with customer expansion revenue. While new customer acquisition is strong, existing customers aren't upgrading their plans or purchasing add-ons as expected. The VP of Customer Success suspects there are predictable patterns in customer behavior that could drive a 40% increase in expansion revenue, but the team lacks visibility into which customers are prime for upselling and what the optimal timing and approach should be.

🎯 Objective
Build an intelligent customer expansion opportunity engine that analyzes usage patterns, engagement metrics, and account characteristics to identify high-potential upsell opportunities and generate personalized expansion strategies for the customer success team.

🧩 Technical Requirement
Create a comprehensive data analysis and recommendation system that processes multi-dimensional customer data, applies statistical modeling to identify expansion readiness signals, and generates actionable playbooks with timing and approach recommendations.

TOOL FOCUS FOR TODAY:
Mixed Tools (Claude Code + Integration)

📂 Inputs
- Customer usage analytics (12 months of feature utilization data)
- Account information (industry, size, current plan, tenure)
- Support ticket history and satisfaction scores
- Previous expansion attempt outcomes (successful/failed)
- Product pricing and feature matrix
- Customer success team interaction logs

📈 Business Deliverables
- Top 50 expansion-ready accounts ranked by opportunity value and success probability
- Personalized expansion playbooks with optimal timing, messaging, and product recommendations
- Revenue opportunity analysis showing potential ARR increase by customer segment
- Customer success team dashboard with real-time opportunity tracking
- Implementation roadmap for systematic expansion revenue growth

🧠 Business Impact
This system transforms reactive customer success into proactive revenue generation. By identifying expansion opportunities 30-45 days before customers naturally hit usage limits, the company can guide strategic conversations rather than reactive upgrade requests. The predictive approach reduces customer success workload while improving expansion revenue predictability and customer satisfaction through proactive value delivery.

🛠 Allowed Tools
Claude Code, Supabase/SQLite for data modeling, Make.com/n8n for automation workflows, spreadsheets for analysis, simple dashboard creation tools

⏱ Time Box
Target: 90-120 minutes

📁 Portfolio Artefact
Customer expansion intelligence system with live opportunity dashboard, expansion playbook generator, and revenue impact calculator - demonstrating advanced customer analytics and revenue operations expertise.

🎓 Skills Trained
- Customer lifecycle analytics and expansion modeling
- Predictive revenue analytics and opportunity scoring
- Customer success automation and workflow optimization
- Multi-factor customer health assessment
- Revenue operations and growth strategy implementation

🧪 Success Criteria
1. Expansion opportunity scoring model identifies accounts with 70%+ accuracy for successful upsells
2. Personalized playbooks generated for each high-opportunity account with specific timing and approach
3. Revenue impact calculator shows potential ARR increase by implementation timeline
4. Automated workflow flags new expansion opportunities within 48 hours of qualifying triggers
5. Dashboard provides actionable insights that customer success team can immediately implement

🚀 Stretch Goals
- Integrate churn risk analysis to prioritize retention-focused expansion opportunities
- Build automated email sequence suggestions for nurturing expansion prospects
- Create competitive analysis component showing win/loss patterns against specific competitors
- Add customer health scoring that combines expansion potential with retention risk
