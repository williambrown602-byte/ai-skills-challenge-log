# DAILY PROFESSIONAL AI CHALLENGE – May 20, 2026

🏢 **Business Scenario**
You're the new analytics lead at a fast-growing B2B software company that's struggling with subscription revenue predictability. The CEO needs reliable forecasting for board meetings, but current revenue projections have been off by 15-25% each quarter. Sales, customer success, and finance teams all use different metrics and assumptions. With Series C funding discussions starting next month, the company needs a unified, data-driven revenue intelligence system that can accurately predict ARR growth and identify the key levers driving subscription performance.

🎯 **Objective**
Build an intelligent subscription revenue analytics and forecasting system that consolidates multi-source data to predict quarterly ARR with 90%+ accuracy and generates actionable growth recommendations for executive decision-making.

🧩 **Technical Requirement**
Create a comprehensive revenue forecasting engine that processes subscription data, customer lifecycle metrics, and market indicators to generate predictive models with confidence intervals and scenario planning capabilities.

**TOOL FOCUS FOR TODAY:**
Mixed Tools (Claude Code + Integration)

📂 **Inputs**
- 24 months of subscription transaction data (5,000+ customers)
- Customer usage analytics and feature adoption metrics
- Sales pipeline data with deal stages and probability scores
- Support ticket volume and customer satisfaction scores
- Churn events with cancellation reasons and customer characteristics
- Product pricing changes and plan migration history

📈 **Business Deliverables**
- Quarterly ARR forecast with 95% confidence intervals for next 4 quarters
- Top 15 revenue growth drivers ranked by impact and controllability
- Customer segment analysis showing expansion opportunities worth $2.8M+
- Churn risk mitigation plan targeting 200+ highest-risk accounts
- Executive dashboard with real-time revenue health indicators

🧠 **Business Impact**
Transforms reactive revenue management into predictive intelligence that enables proactive growth strategies. Provides board-ready financial projections that support funding discussions while identifying specific operational improvements worth $3-5M in protected/accelerated revenue.

🛠 **Allowed Tools**
Claude Code (primary), Spreadsheet integration, Database queries, API connections, Dashboard creation tools, Statistical analysis libraries

⏱ **Time Box**
Target: 90-120 minutes

📁 **Portfolio Artefact**
Complete revenue intelligence system with forecasting models, executive dashboard, and strategic growth recommendations - demonstrates advanced business analytics and SaaS metrics expertise for senior analyst/director roles.

🎓 **Skills Trained**
- SaaS revenue analytics and subscription metrics mastery
- Advanced predictive modeling with uncertainty quantification
- Multi-source business data integration and correlation analysis
- Executive-level financial communication and board presentation skills
- Strategic business intelligence and growth optimization

🧪 **Success Criteria**
1. Revenue forecast accuracy within 5% margin of error for next quarter
2. Customer segmentation identifies $2M+ in expansion opportunities
3. Churn prediction model achieves 80%+ precision on high-risk accounts
4. Growth driver analysis provides 5+ actionable strategic recommendations
5. Executive dashboard delivers insights in under 30 seconds for any time period

🚀 **Stretch Goals**
- Build automated alert system for revenue anomalies and opportunities
- Create competitive benchmarking analysis against industry standards
- Develop pricing optimization recommendations based on customer value analysis
- Generate investor presentation slides with growth trajectory projections
- Design A/B testing framework for validating growth initiatives
