DAILY PROFESSIONAL AI CHALLENGE – June 03, 2026

🏢 Business Scenario
You're the lead analyst for a mid-sized B2B SaaS company ($45M ARR) experiencing concerning trends: customer acquisition costs have risen 35% over 18 months while trial-to-paid conversion rates dropped from 18% to 12%. The VP of Revenue Operations needs data-driven insights to present to the board next week, showing exactly where the funnel is breaking and what strategic interventions will restore healthy growth metrics.

🎯 Objective
Build an intelligent revenue funnel optimization system that analyzes the complete customer journey from initial touchpoint to paid conversion, identifies specific breakdown points, predicts conversion probability for active prospects, and generates strategic recommendations to restore conversion rates to 18%+ within 6 months.

🧩 Technical Requirement
Create a multi-stage funnel analysis engine with statistical significance testing, cohort analysis capabilities, and predictive modeling to identify high-impact optimization opportunities across the entire customer acquisition process.

TOOL FOCUS FOR TODAY:
Primarily Claude Code

📂 Inputs
- 24 months of marketing touchpoint data (source, channel, campaign, cost per lead)
- Trial signup data with user attributes (company size, industry, role, geography)
- Product usage analytics during trial periods (feature adoption, session frequency, depth of engagement)
- Sales interaction logs (demo requests, sales calls, follow-up sequences)
- Conversion outcomes and timeline data for all prospects
- Customer success data for converted accounts (expansion, retention, satisfaction)

📈 Business Deliverables
- Funnel stage conversion analysis showing drop-off rates and statistical significance
- Top 200 active prospects ranked by conversion probability with intervention recommendations
- Channel efficiency analysis identifying highest-ROI acquisition sources
- Trial experience optimization recommendations targeting specific user segments
- Strategic action plan with projected impact on conversion rates and CAC reduction
- Board-ready executive summary quantifying revenue impact of recommended changes

🧠 Business Impact
Enables data-driven revenue optimization decisions that could recover $2-3M in annual recurring revenue growth while reducing customer acquisition costs by 25%. Provides executive leadership with clear strategic direction for marketing spend allocation and sales process improvements.

🛠 Allowed Tools
Claude Code (primary), basic spreadsheets for data organization, simple visualization capabilities

⏱ Time Box
Target: 90–120 minutes

📁 Portfolio Artefact
Complete revenue funnel optimization system with predictive conversion model, executive dashboard, and strategic action plan suitable for board presentation and revenue operations implementation.

🎓 Skills Trained
- SaaS revenue analytics and conversion optimization
- Multi-stage funnel analysis with statistical modeling
- Customer journey analytics and behavioral prediction
- Executive-level revenue strategy communication
- Predictive modeling for business growth optimization

🧪 Success Criteria
1. Funnel analysis identifies conversion rates at each stage with statistical confidence levels
2. Predictive model achieves 80%+ accuracy in ranking prospect conversion likelihood
3. Channel analysis quantifies ROI differences with actionable reallocation recommendations
4. Trial optimization suggestions address specific user behavior patterns with measurable impact projections
5. Strategic plan includes timeline, resource requirements, and projected financial outcomes

🚀 Stretch Goals
- Implement cohort analysis showing conversion trends by signup month and user characteristics
- Create automated alert system for prospects showing high conversion probability requiring immediate sales intervention
- Develop competitive win/loss analysis correlating market dynamics with conversion performance
- Generate personalized trial experience recommendations based on user profile and engagement patterns
