DAILY PROFESSIONAL AI CHALLENGE – June 05, 2026

🏢 Business Scenario
You're the newly hired Chief Data Officer at a mid-sized regional bank that has been struggling with credit decisions. The bank's current loan approval process relies heavily on traditional credit scores and manual underwriting, resulting in 28% of approved loans defaulting within 18 months—well above the 12% industry benchmark. The CEO wants to transform lending operations using advanced analytics to reduce risk while maintaining competitive approval rates for qualified borrowers.

🎯 Objective
Build an intelligent credit risk assessment and decision support system that analyzes multi-dimensional borrower data to generate accurate risk predictions and automated lending recommendations that can reduce default rates to industry benchmarks while improving decision speed.

🧩 Technical Requirement
Develop a comprehensive credit risk modeling system that processes borrower financial data, generates risk scores with confidence intervals, and creates automated decision workflows with explainable recommendations for loan officers.

TOOL FOCUS FOR TODAY:
Primarily Claude Code

📂 Inputs
- 18 months of loan performance data (2,400 loans with outcomes)
- Borrower financial profiles (income, debt ratios, employment history, assets)
- Industry benchmark data for default rates by loan type and borrower segment
- Current manual underwriting guidelines and approval criteria
- Economic indicators and market conditions data

📈 Business Deliverables
- Advanced credit risk scoring model with 85%+ accuracy in default prediction
- Automated loan approval/decline recommendation system with confidence scores
- Top 150 highest-risk existing loans requiring immediate portfolio review
- Risk-adjusted pricing recommendations for different borrower segments
- Loan officer decision support dashboard with explainable AI recommendations

🧠 Business Impact
Enable data-driven lending decisions that protect the bank's assets while maintaining competitive market position. Reduce manual underwriting time by 70%, improve risk assessment accuracy, and create systematic approach to credit decisions that can be defended to regulators and auditors.

🛠 Allowed Tools
- Claude Code (primary)
- Python libraries for financial modeling and statistics
- Data visualization tools for executive reporting
- Spreadsheet tools for data input/output

⏱ Time Box
Target: 90–120 minutes

📁 Portfolio Artefact
Credit risk assessment system with predictive models, automated decision framework, and executive dashboard—demonstrating advanced financial analytics and regulatory-compliant decision support capabilities.

🎓 Skills Trained
- Financial risk modeling and credit analytics
- Banking industry regulatory knowledge and compliance
- Multi-factor predictive algorithms with confidence intervals
- Automated decision support system design
- Executive-level financial communication and risk management

🧪 Success Criteria
1. Credit risk model achieves 85%+ accuracy in predicting defaults
2. Automated recommendation system covers 80%+ of loan applications
3. Risk scoring system provides explainable factors for each decision
4. Portfolio review identifies high-risk loans with specific intervention recommendations
5. Executive dashboard clearly communicates risk levels and strategic recommendations

🚀 Stretch Goals
- Create loan pricing optimization model based on risk-adjusted returns
- Develop early warning system for existing loan portfolio monitoring
- Build regulatory stress testing scenarios for portfolio resilience
- Generate competitive analysis showing improved risk-return profile
- Create board presentation quantifying potential improvement in bank profitability
