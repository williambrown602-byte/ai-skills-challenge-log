DAILY PROFESSIONAL AI CHALLENGE – March 26, 2026

🏢 Business Scenario
You're working with a mid-market SaaS company ($50M ARR) that's experiencing customer satisfaction issues. The company receives 500+ support tickets monthly across email, chat, and phone channels, but has no systematic way to understand customer sentiment or identify systemic product issues. Customer success teams are overwhelmed manually categorizing feedback, and product teams lack visibility into recurring pain points that could guide their roadmap decisions.

🎯 Objective
Build an intelligent customer feedback analysis system that automatically processes multi-channel customer communications, extracts sentiment and themes, identifies product improvement opportunities, and generates actionable insights for both customer success and product development teams.

🧩 Technical Requirement
Develop a comprehensive text analysis and sentiment classification system that can process unstructured customer communications from multiple sources and generate structured business intelligence outputs.

TOOL FOCUS FOR TODAY:
Mixed Tools (Claude Code + Integration)

📂 Inputs
- 300 customer support tickets (email/chat transcripts)
- 150 customer success call transcripts
- 200 product review/feedback entries
- Customer account data (plan type, tenure, revenue)
- Product feature usage metrics per customer

📈 Business Deliverables
- Automated sentiment scoring system for all customer communications
- Top 15 customer pain points ranked by frequency and business impact
- Product roadmap recommendations with customer demand quantification
- Customer health risk assessment based on communication sentiment trends
- Executive dashboard showing customer satisfaction trends and intervention priorities

🧠 Business Impact
This system transforms reactive customer support into proactive customer success by identifying issues before they escalate to churn. Product teams get data-driven roadmap guidance, potentially increasing feature adoption by 25% and reducing support volume by 30%. Customer success teams can intervene early with at-risk accounts, protecting $2-5M in annual recurring revenue.

🛠 Allowed Tools
- Claude Code for text processing and analysis
- Supabase or SQLite for data storage
- Spreadsheet tools for data manipulation
- Simple dashboard/visualization tools
- Email/notification systems for alerts

⏱ Time Box
Target: 90-120 minutes

📁 Portfolio Artefact
Customer Intelligence Analytics System with sentiment analysis engine, theme extraction capabilities, and executive reporting dashboard that demonstrates advanced NLP skills and business intelligence development.

🎓 Skills Trained
- Natural language processing and sentiment analysis
- Customer experience analytics and insights generation
- Multi-source data integration and processing
- Product management and customer success intelligence
- Executive-level reporting and business communication

🧪 Success Criteria
1. Sentiment analysis system accurately categorizes feedback as positive/neutral/negative with confidence scores
2. Theme extraction identifies at least 10 distinct customer pain points with frequency rankings
3. Customer risk scoring combines sentiment trends with account value to prioritize interventions
4. Product improvement recommendations include demand quantification and revenue impact estimates
5. Executive dashboard provides actionable insights that customer success and product teams can immediately act upon

- Create competitor mention detection and analysis
