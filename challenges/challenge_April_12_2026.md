DAILY PROFESSIONAL AI CHALLENGE – April 12, 2026

🏢 Business Scenario
You work for a mid-sized digital marketing agency managing 85+ client accounts. The agency struggles with campaign performance inconsistency - some campaigns deliver 300%+ ROAS while others barely break even. Partners need to understand what separates high-performing campaigns from underperformers to improve overall client retention (currently 72%) and justify premium pricing. Manual campaign analysis takes 2-3 days per client and insights often come too late to salvage declining performance.

🎯 Objective
Build an intelligent campaign performance analysis system that automatically evaluates multi-channel marketing data, identifies success patterns, and generates actionable optimization recommendations to transform campaign management from reactive troubleshooting into proactive strategic optimization.

🧩 Technical Requirement
Create a multi-dimensional campaign analysis engine that processes complex marketing attribution data, performs statistical correlation analysis between campaign elements and performance outcomes, and generates ranked optimization recommendations with projected ROI impact.

TOOL FOCUS FOR TODAY:
Mixed Tools (Claude Code + Integration)

📂 Inputs
- 18 months of campaign performance data (impressions, clicks, conversions, spend by channel)
- Client industry classifications and seasonal patterns
- Creative performance metrics and A/B test results
- Account manager performance ratings and client satisfaction scores
- Competitive spend estimates and market share data

📈 Business Deliverables
- Campaign Performance Diagnostic Report identifying top 15 success drivers
- Client risk assessment showing accounts requiring immediate optimization attention
- Channel allocation optimization recommendations with projected ROAS improvements
- Account manager coaching priorities based on campaign performance patterns
- Strategic pricing justification framework linking performance capabilities to fee structure

🧠 Business Impact
Enables agency to systematically replicate high-performing campaign strategies across all accounts, reducing client churn by 25% while supporting 15-20% premium pricing through demonstrated performance superiority. Transforms subjective campaign management into data-driven strategic advantage.

🛠 Allowed Tools
Claude Code, Google Sheets, Make.com, Basic BI visualization tools, Email automation platforms

⏱ Time Box
Target: 90-120 minutes

📁 Portfolio Artefact
Campaign optimization intelligence system with performance prediction capabilities and strategic recommendations framework suitable for agency new business presentations.

- Pattern recognition in campaign performance data
- Strategic pricing and value proposition development
- Client relationship management through data insights
- Digital marketing industry business intelligence
