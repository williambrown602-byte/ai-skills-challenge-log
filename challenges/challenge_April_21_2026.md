DAILY PROFESSIONAL AI CHALLENGE – April 21, 2026

🏢 Business Scenario
You're a data analyst at a growing B2B software company that's struggling with inefficient lead qualification. The sales team spends 60% of their time on leads that never convert, while high-potential prospects slip through due to inconsistent follow-up timing. Marketing generates 2,000+ leads monthly across 6 channels, but only 8% become qualified opportunities. The VP of Revenue Operations needs a systematic approach to transform lead management from gut-feel to data-driven precision.

🎯 Objective
Build an intelligent lead lifecycle analytics system that identifies conversion patterns, predicts optimal engagement timing, and generates actionable lead prioritization strategies to increase qualified lead rates from 8% to 15% while reducing sales qualification time by 50%.

🧩 Technical Requirement
Create a comprehensive lead scoring algorithm that processes historical lead data, identifies behavioral patterns indicating purchase intent, and generates predictive engagement recommendations with confidence scoring.

TOOL FOCUS FOR TODAY:
Primarily Claude Code

📂 Inputs
- 18 months of lead data (source, company size, industry, engagement history, conversion outcomes)
- Sales activity logs with timing and interaction types
- Marketing campaign performance data across channels
- Lead scoring attempts from previous systems
- Current qualification criteria and sales process documentation

📈 Business Deliverables
- Dynamic lead scoring model with 0-100 qualification probability
- Optimal engagement timing predictions for different lead segments
- Top 200 current leads ranked by conversion probability with recommended actions
- Channel performance analysis showing lead quality by source
- Sales team action plan with personalized lead priority lists

🧠 Business Impact
Enables sales reps to focus exclusively on high-probability prospects, dramatically improving productivity and revenue per rep. Provides marketing with clear feedback on channel effectiveness, enabling budget optimization. Creates predictable pipeline flow that supports accurate revenue forecasting and strategic planning.

🛠 Allowed Tools

- Python/statistical libraries for modeling
- Data visualization tools
- Spreadsheet integration for sales team delivery
