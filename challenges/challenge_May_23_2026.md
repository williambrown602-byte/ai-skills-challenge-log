DAILY PROFESSIONAL AI CHALLENGE – May 23, 2026

🏢 Business Scenario
You're supporting a B2B technology company that's struggling with their customer support efficiency. They have a distributed team of 45 agents handling 3,200+ monthly tickets across email, chat, and phone channels. Current resolution times average 3.8 days, with significant variation between agents (1.2 to 8.5 days). Management needs to understand what drives efficient resolution and optimize agent performance to reduce average resolution time to under 2.5 days while maintaining quality scores above 4.2/5.

🎯 Objective
Build an intelligent support performance optimization system that analyzes agent productivity patterns, identifies efficiency drivers, and generates actionable coaching recommendations to transform support operations from reactive management to data-driven performance excellence.

🧩 Technical Requirement
Create a comprehensive agent performance analysis engine that processes support ticket data, identifies success patterns, generates coaching priorities, and builds predictive models for resolution time optimization.

TOOL FOCUS FOR TODAY:
Primarily Claude Code

📂 Inputs
Assume access to:
- 18 months of support ticket data (ticket type, channel, agent, resolution time, customer satisfaction scores)
- Agent information (tenure, training completion, specializations, workload)
- Customer data (account size, product usage, support history)
- Quality assurance scores and customer feedback
- Escalation patterns and manager intervention data

📈 Business Deliverables
- Agent performance ranking with specific improvement opportunities for each underperformer
- Support efficiency playbook identifying top 10 practices of high-performing agents
- Personalized coaching recommendations for each agent with projected impact on resolution times
- Channel optimization strategy showing optimal routing and staffing recommendations
- Executive dashboard with KPIs and 90-day performance improvement roadmap

🧠 Business Impact
Enable the company to reduce support costs by 30% while improving customer satisfaction, directly impacting retention rates and reducing escalation to senior management. Demonstrate measurable ROI through improved agent productivity and reduced customer churn.

🛠 Allowed Tools
Claude Code (primary), basic visualization tools, spreadsheet functions for data validation

⏱ Time Box
Target: 90-120 minutes

📁 Portfolio Artefact
Complete support optimization system with agent coaching framework, performance analytics dashboard, and executive presentation showing projected business impact

🎓 Skills Trained
- Customer support analytics and operational efficiency
- Multi-factor performance analysis and agent optimization
- Predictive modeling for service delivery improvement
- Executive-level operational reporting and coaching framework development
- Customer experience correlation analysis and retention strategy

🧪 Success Criteria
1. Agent ranking system correctly identifies top and bottom performers with statistical significance
2. Coaching recommendations are specific, actionable, and tied to measurable outcomes
3. Channel optimization shows clear efficiency gains with supporting data
4. Resolution time predictions achieve 80%+ accuracy for capacity planning
5. Executive summary quantifies potential business impact with realistic timelines

🚀 Stretch Goals
- Build agent workload balancing recommendations accounting for skill specializations
- Create customer satisfaction prediction model based on agent assignment
- Develop automated escalation triggers based on ticket complexity and agent capability
- Generate hiring profile recommendations based on high-performer characteristics
