DAILY PROFESSIONAL AI CHALLENGE – April 24, 2026

🏢 Business Scenario
You're the analytics consultant for a mid-sized regional bank struggling with loan default rates that have increased from 3.2% to 5.8% over the past 18 months. The credit committee meets weekly but relies on outdated spreadsheet-based risk assessments that take 2-3 days to prepare. With $240M in active loans across commercial, residential, and personal lending portfolios, improving risk prediction accuracy by even 1% could save $2.4M annually in avoided defaults.

🎯 Objective
Build an intelligent credit risk assessment and portfolio monitoring system that transforms manual risk analysis into automated, predictive insights. Generate real-time risk scores, identify high-risk loan concentrations, and provide actionable recommendations for the weekly credit committee meeting.

🧩 Technical Requirement
Develop a comprehensive risk scoring algorithm that processes multi-dimensional loan data, applies statistical models for default prediction, and creates executive-ready risk visualizations with portfolio-level insights.
