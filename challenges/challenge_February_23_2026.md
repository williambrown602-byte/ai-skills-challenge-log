🏢 Business Scenario
You're a data analyst at a mid-sized consulting firm where partners are struggling with inconsistent proposal pricing. Currently, pricing decisions rely on gut instinct and basic spreadsheets, leading to 40% variance in hourly rates for similar engagements. The managing partner wants a sophisticated pricing intelligence system that analyzes historical project data to recommend optimal pricing strategies and identify revenue optimization opportunities across different practice areas.

🎯 Objective
Build an intelligent pricing analytics system that processes historical engagement data to generate data-driven pricing recommendations, identify underpriced opportunities, and create a dynamic pricing framework that partners can use during client negotiations.

🧩 Technical Requirement
Develop advanced statistical models for pricing optimization, create automated scenario analysis tools, and build interactive pricing calculators that factor in multiple business variables including client industry, engagement complexity, team seniority, and market positioning.

TOOL FOCUS FOR TODAY:
Primarily Claude Code

📈 Business Deliverables

- Interactive pricing scenario calculator for partner use

Enable data-driven pricing decisions that could increase average engagement value by 12-18%. Reduce pricing inconsistencies and improve competitive positioning. Create standardized pricing methodology that junior partners can confidently use. Identify $2-3M in potential annual revenue opportunities through optimized pricing strategies.
