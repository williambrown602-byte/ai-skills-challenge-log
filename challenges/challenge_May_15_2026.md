DAILY PROFESSIONAL AI CHALLENGE – May 15, 2026

🏢 Business Scenario
You work for a rapidly growing digital marketing agency that manages $8M in annual ad spend across 120+ client accounts. The agency has experienced explosive growth but is struggling with client satisfaction issues - NPS has dropped from 67 to 41 over 8 months. The leadership team suspects campaign performance inconsistencies, communication gaps, and resource allocation problems are contributing to client churn risk. With 15% client churn threatening $1.2M in annual revenue, you need to transform scattered client performance data into actionable insights for immediate intervention.

🎯 Objective
Build an intelligent client health monitoring and optimization system that identifies at-risk accounts, analyzes performance patterns across the client portfolio, and generates strategic retention recommendations with measurable ROI projections to restore client satisfaction and protect revenue.

🧩 Technical Requirement
Create a comprehensive client analytics engine that processes multi-dimensional performance data (campaign metrics, communication frequency, account profitability, satisfaction indicators) to generate risk scores, identify success patterns, and automate strategic intervention recommendations.

TOOL FOCUS FOR TODAY:
Primarily Claude Code

📂 Inputs
- Client performance dataset (120 accounts, 18 months of campaign metrics)
- Account profitability data with monthly revenue and margin figures
- Communication tracking logs (emails, calls, meetings by account)
- Client satisfaction scores from quarterly surveys
- Account manager assignments and workload distribution
- Campaign type classifications and industry verticals

📈 Business Deliverables
- Top 25 highest-risk clients ranked by churn probability with specific intervention strategies
- Client health scoring dashboard (0-100 scale) with trend analysis and early warning indicators
- Account manager workload optimization recommendations showing rebalancing opportunities
- Performance pattern analysis identifying characteristics of high-satisfaction vs. at-risk accounts
- Strategic client retention plan with 90-day action items and projected revenue protection

🧠 Business Impact
Transform reactive client management into predictive relationship optimization. Enable account managers to focus efforts on highest-impact opportunities while preventing churn before it occurs. Demonstrate data-driven agency capabilities that justify premium pricing and improve client retention economics.

🛠 Allowed Tools
Claude Code (primary), basic data visualization, statistical analysis libraries, any built-in calculation tools

⏱ Time Box
Target: 90-120 minutes

📁 Portfolio Artefact
Client health monitoring system with predictive analytics, strategic retention playbook, and executive presentation showing methodology and business impact projections

🎓 Skills Trained
- Client relationship analytics and churn prediction modeling
- Digital marketing industry business intelligence
- Multi-factor risk assessment and scoring algorithms
- Strategic account management optimization
- Executive-level client retention strategy development

🧪 Success Criteria
1. Generate accurate client risk scores incorporating performance, communication, and satisfaction data
2. Identify top 25 at-risk accounts with specific, actionable intervention recommendations
3. Create account manager workload optimization showing improved client coverage
4. Produce pattern analysis revealing key differentiators of successful vs. struggling accounts
5. Deliver executive-ready retention strategy with clear ROI projections and implementation timeline

🚀 Stretch Goals
- Build predictive model forecasting 6-month client lifetime value by risk category
- Create automated early warning system template for ongoing client health monitoring
- Generate competitive differentiation analysis showing how improved client satisfaction supports premium positioning
