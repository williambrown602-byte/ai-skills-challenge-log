DAILY PROFESSIONAL AI CHALLENGE – March 17, 2026

🏢 Business Scenario
You're working as a data analyst at a mid-sized e-commerce company experiencing rapid growth but struggling with product catalog management. The merchandising team currently spends 3-4 hours daily manually categorizing new products, writing descriptions, and identifying cross-sell opportunities. With 200+ new SKUs added weekly, this manual process is becoming unsustainable and inconsistent. The CMO wants an intelligent system to automate product enrichment and generate strategic merchandising insights to improve conversion rates and average order value.

🎯 Objective
Build an intelligent product catalog optimization system that automatically enriches product data, generates optimized descriptions, identifies cross-sell opportunities, and creates merchandising recommendations to reduce manual work by 75% while improving product discoverability and sales performance.

🧩 Technical Requirement
Develop a comprehensive product data processing pipeline with natural language generation, similarity analysis, and recommendation algorithms that can handle batch processing of product catalogs.

TOOL FOCUS FOR TODAY:
Primarily Claude Code

📂 Inputs
- Raw product catalog CSV with basic info (name, category, price, basic specs)
- Historical sales transaction data showing product purchase patterns
- Customer review snippets for existing similar products
- Competitor product descriptions for benchmarking

📈 Business Deliverables
- Automated product description generator with SEO optimization
- Cross-sell recommendation matrix showing high-affinity product pairs
- Category optimization suggestions for improved product placement
- Merchandising strategy report identifying high-potential products
- Quality scoring system for existing product listings

🧠 Business Impact
This system would save the merchandising team 15-20 hours weekly while improving product page conversion rates through better descriptions and cross-selling. Enhanced product categorization and SEO optimization could increase organic search traffic by 25-30%, directly impacting revenue growth and operational efficiency.

🛠 Allowed Tools
- Claude Code for data processing and NLP tasks
- Python libraries for text analysis and similarity calculations
- CSV/JSON for data handling
- Basic statistical analysis for sales pattern identification

⏱ Time Box
Target: 90-120 minutes

📁 Portfolio Artefact
Complete product catalog optimization system with sample outputs demonstrating automated enrichment, cross-sell analysis, and merchandising insights that showcase e-commerce and retail analytics capabilities.

🎓 Skills Trained
- Natural language processing and text generation
- Product recommendation algorithms and similarity analysis
- E-commerce analytics and merchandising strategy
- Automated content creation and SEO optimization
- Retail business intelligence and conversion optimization

1. System successfully generates compelling product descriptions for 50+ products in under 5 minutes
2. Cross-sell recommendations achieve logical product pairing with confidence scores
3. Category optimization suggestions identify at least 10 mis-categorized products
4. Quality scoring system accurately flags low-performing product listings
5. Merchandising report provides 5 actionable insights for revenue improvement
