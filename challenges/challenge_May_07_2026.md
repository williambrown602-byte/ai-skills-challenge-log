DAILY PROFESSIONAL AI CHALLENGE – May 07, 2026

🏢 Business Scenario
You're the lead business analyst at a mid-sized healthcare technology company that provides practice management software to 450+ medical practices. The company has been experiencing concerning trends: customer support tickets have increased 180% over 6 months, customer satisfaction scores have dropped from 4.2 to 3.1 stars, and monthly churn has risen from 2.1% to 4.8%. The CEO needs a comprehensive analysis to understand what's driving customer dissatisfaction and a strategic action plan to restore service quality before the upcoming Series B funding round in 10 weeks.

🎯 Objective
Build an intelligent customer experience optimization system that identifies root causes of declining satisfaction, predicts which customers are at highest risk of churning, and generates a prioritized action plan with projected impact on customer retention and satisfaction recovery.

🧩 Technical Requirement
Create a multi-dimensional analysis engine that processes customer feedback, support interactions, and usage patterns to generate predictive insights and automated intervention recommendations.

TOOL FOCUS FOR TODAY:
Mixed Tools (Claude Code + Integration)

📂 Inputs
- 18 months of support ticket data (12,500+ tickets with categories, resolution times, satisfaction ratings)
- Customer satisfaction survey responses (3,200 responses across 6 months)
- Product usage analytics (feature adoption rates, session duration, error logs)
- Account information (plan types, tenure, practice size, specialty)
- Churn data (180 churned customers with exit survey feedback)

📈 Business Deliverables
1. Customer health risk dashboard identifying top 100 at-risk accounts with churn probability scores
2. Root cause analysis report ranking the top 10 satisfaction drivers by business impact
3. Support optimization recommendations with projected ticket volume reduction
4. Customer intervention playbook with personalized retention strategies
5. Executive presentation with 90-day recovery roadmap and financial projections

🧠 Business Impact
This system transforms reactive customer service into proactive experience management, potentially preventing $1.2M in annual recurring revenue loss while demonstrating operational excellence to investors. The insights enable data-driven resource allocation and strategic product improvements that could restore customer satisfaction to industry-leading levels.
