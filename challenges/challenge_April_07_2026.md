DAILY PROFESSIONAL AI CHALLENGE – April 07, 2026

🏢 Business Scenario
You're a data analyst at a fast-growing healthcare technology company that provides patient management software to 150+ medical practices. The Customer Success team is overwhelmed manually analyzing support tickets, NPS scores, and usage patterns to identify which clients need proactive intervention. Currently, 23% of clients churn within 18 months, and the team can only proactively reach out to 15-20 clients per month due to manual analysis bottlenecks.

🎯 Objective
Build an intelligent customer health scoring system that automatically processes multi-source customer data to generate prioritized intervention lists and actionable customer success strategies. Reduce manual analysis time from 3 days to 30 minutes while improving early churn detection accuracy.

🧩 Technical Requirement
Create a comprehensive customer health analytics engine that processes support tickets, usage metrics, and satisfaction scores to generate risk-weighted customer segments with specific intervention recommendations.

TOOL FOCUS FOR TODAY:
Mixed Tools (Claude Code + Integration)

📂 Inputs
- Customer usage data (login frequency, feature adoption, user growth)
- Support ticket history with resolution times and satisfaction ratings
- NPS survey responses and feedback comments
- Account details (contract value, implementation date, team size)
- Customer success interaction logs

📈 Business Deliverables
- Top 25 at-risk customers ranked by churn probability with specific risk factors
- Automated customer health scoring system (0-100 scale) with trend analysis
- Personalized intervention playbooks for each risk category
- Monthly customer portfolio health report showing segment distributions
- ROI projection showing potential revenue retention from proactive interventions

🧠 Business Impact
Enable the Customer Success team to shift from reactive firefighting to strategic, data-driven relationship management. Potential to reduce churn by 35% while scaling customer success operations without proportional headcount increases. Creates competitive advantage through superior customer retention metrics.

🛠 Allowed Tools
- Claude Code for core analytics engine
- Supabase/SQLite for data management
- Spreadsheet tools for data preparation
- Make.com/n8n for workflow automation
- Simple dashboard creation tools

⏱ Time Box
Target: 90-120 minutes

📁 Portfolio Artefact
Customer Health Intelligence System with live scoring dashboard, intervention playbooks, and ROI projection model demonstrating measurable business impact on customer retention.

🎓 Skills Trained
- Customer lifecycle analytics and churn prediction modeling
- Multi-source data integration and health scoring algorithms
- Healthcare technology industry knowledge and customer success optimization
- Predictive analytics for customer relationship management
- Revenue retention strategy and business impact measurement

1. Health scoring algorithm incorporates at least 5 key customer metrics with weighted importance
2. Risk categorization system produces actionable intervention priorities for CS team
3. Automated analysis reduces manual work by 85%+ while maintaining accuracy
4. Intervention playbooks are specific and actionable for each risk level
5. ROI projections show clear business case for systematic customer health monitoring
