DAILY PROFESSIONAL AI CHALLENGE – March 21, 2026

🏢 Business Scenario
You're working with a mid-sized SaaS company (150 employees) that's struggling with inefficient meeting culture. The VP of Operations has identified that employees spend 40% of their time in meetings, with only 60% rated as "valuable" in recent surveys. The company wants to optimize meeting effectiveness, reduce meeting overhead, and improve decision-making velocity across departments.

🎯 Objective
Build an intelligent meeting optimization system that analyzes calendar data and meeting patterns to identify inefficiencies, generate meeting consolidation recommendations, and create a data-driven meeting governance framework that could reduce meeting time by 25% while improving decision outcomes.

🧩 Technical Requirement
Create an automated system that processes meeting calendar data, performs pattern analysis on recurring meetings, and builds optimization algorithms for meeting scheduling and attendee selection based on decision-making effectiveness metrics.

TOOL FOCUS FOR TODAY:
Mixed Tools (Claude Code + Integration)

📂 Inputs
- 6 months of calendar data (meeting frequency, duration, attendees, types)
- Employee survey data on meeting effectiveness ratings
- Department structure and reporting hierarchies
- Sample meeting costs data (salary-based meeting cost calculations)
- Decision tracking data (decisions made, time to implementation)

📈 Business Deliverables
- Meeting efficiency audit showing cost and time waste analysis
- Automated meeting consolidation recommendations with projected time savings
- Optimized recurring meeting schedule for each department
- Meeting governance scorecard with effectiveness metrics
- Executive dashboard tracking meeting ROI and productivity gains

🧠 Business Impact
Demonstrate ability to tackle organizational productivity challenges through data-driven analysis. This showcases skills in operational consulting, change management, and quantitative business analysis - highly valued capabilities for management consulting, operations roles, and internal strategy positions. The solution directly impacts bottom-line productivity and employee satisfaction.

🛠 Allowed Tools
- Claude Code (primary analysis and algorithms)
- Spreadsheet software (calendar data processing)
- Simple dashboard creation tool
- Email/calendar integration APIs (optional)
- Basic visualization tools

⏱ Time Box
Target: 90-120 minutes

📁 Portfolio Artefact
Meeting Optimization System with executive summary, interactive efficiency calculator, and governance framework - perfect for demonstrating analytical problem-solving and operational improvement capabilities to potential employers.

🎓 Skills Trained
- Organizational productivity analysis
- Calendar and time-management analytics
- Business process optimization
- Change management and governance design
- ROI calculation and cost-benefit analysis

🧪 Success Criteria
1. System identifies at least 15 specific meeting optimization opportunities
2. Cost savings projections show methodology with realistic salary-based calculations
3. Governance framework includes measurable KPIs and implementation timeline
4. Analysis segments findings by department and meeting type effectiveness
5. Recommendations include both quick wins and strategic organizational changes

🚀 Stretch Goals
- Build meeting agenda optimization suggestions based on decision patterns
- Create automated meeting scheduler that optimizes for participant availability and decision-making effectiveness
- Develop meeting ROI calculator that managers can use ongoing
- Generate personalized meeting efficiency reports for individual employees
