DAILY PROFESSIONAL AI CHALLENGE – April 05, 2026

🏢 Business Scenario
You're the senior data analyst at a mid-sized pharmaceutical company facing increasing pressure from regulatory bodies and internal stakeholders to demonstrate comprehensive drug safety monitoring. The company has 12 approved medications across different therapeutic areas, with adverse event reports coming from multiple sources (clinical trials, post-market surveillance, physician reports, patient portals). Currently, safety signal detection takes 3-4 weeks of manual analysis, and the company has missed two important safety trends in the past year that resulted in costly label updates and regulatory scrutiny.

🎯 Objective
Build an intelligent pharmacovigilance analytics system that automatically processes adverse event data to detect safety signals, assess risk levels, and generate regulatory-compliant safety reports with actionable recommendations for risk mitigation.

🧩 Technical Requirement
Create a comprehensive adverse event analysis engine that performs statistical signal detection using multiple algorithms, generates risk-benefit assessments, and produces automated regulatory reports with trend analysis and intervention recommendations.

TOOL FOCUS FOR TODAY:
Primarily Claude Code

📂 Inputs
- 18 months of adverse event reports (12,000 cases across all products)
- Drug exposure data showing patient populations and usage patterns
- Medical terminology mappings and severity classifications
- Regulatory reporting templates and safety thresholds
- Competitor safety data for benchmarking

📈 Business Deliverables
- Automated safety signal detection system with statistical confidence scoring
- Product-specific risk assessment reports ranking safety concerns by severity and frequency
- Regulatory-ready periodic safety update reports with trend analysis
- Risk mitigation recommendation matrix with implementation priorities
- Executive safety dashboard showing portfolio-wide risk indicators

🧠 Business Impact
Enable proactive safety monitoring that protects patients and reduces regulatory risk. Transform 3-4 week manual processes into same-day automated analysis, prevent costly regulatory actions through early detection, and demonstrate industry-leading pharmacovigilance capabilities to regulators and stakeholders. Potential cost avoidance of $2-5M annually through early risk identification.

🛠 Allowed Tools
Claude Code (primary), spreadsheets for data validation, basic visualization tools for dashboard mockups

⏱ Time Box
Target: 90-120 minutes

📁 Portfolio Artefact
Complete pharmacovigilance analytics system with sample reports, statistical models, and executive dashboard demonstrating pharmaceutical industry expertise and regulatory compliance knowledge.

🎓 Skills Trained
- Pharmaceutical industry regulatory knowledge
- Statistical signal detection and epidemiological analysis
- Medical data processing and safety analytics
- Regulatory compliance reporting and documentation
- Risk assessment methodology and executive communication

🧪 Success Criteria
1. Signal detection algorithm identifies safety trends with 85%+ sensitivity
2. Risk assessment correctly prioritizes high-impact safety concerns
3. Automated reports meet regulatory formatting and content requirements
4. System processes 12,000+ adverse event records in under 5 minutes
5. Executive dashboard clearly communicates portfolio risk status and trends

🚀 Stretch Goals
- Implement natural language processing for adverse event narrative analysis
- Create predictive models for emerging safety signal identification
- Build automated competitor benchmarking with market safety context
- Design patient communication templates for identified safety concerns
