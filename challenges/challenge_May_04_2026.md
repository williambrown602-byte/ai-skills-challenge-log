🏢 Business Scenario
You're the VP of Operations at a rapidly expanding fintech startup that processes $2.8B in annual transactions across 45,000 merchants. The company has grown 340% in 18 months, but this growth has created serious operational blind spots. Transaction disputes have increased 180%, resolution times have ballooned to 21 days (from 8 days), and merchant satisfaction has dropped to 67%. The CEO needs a comprehensive fraud detection and dispute resolution optimization system before the next board meeting in 10 days to demonstrate operational maturity and scalability.

🎯 Objective
Build an intelligent transaction monitoring and dispute resolution system that identifies fraud patterns, predicts dispute likelihood, optimizes case routing to specialists, and generates strategic recommendations to reduce resolution times to under 10 days while improving merchant retention.

🧩 Technical Requirement
Develop a multi-layered analytics engine that processes transaction data to detect anomalies, scores dispute risk probability, creates automated case prioritization, and generates predictive insights for operational decision-making.

TOOL FOCUS FOR TODAY:

- Fraud investigation outcomes and pattern classifications

📈 Business Deliverables

- Optimal case routing recommendations to reduce specialist workload imbalance by 40%
- Merchant risk assessment showing top 200 accounts requiring proactive outreach
- Strategic operational plan with resource allocation recommendations and projected ROI

🧠 Business Impact
Transform chaotic dispute management into predictive, data-driven operations that protect revenue while improving merchant relationships. Demonstrate to investors that the company can scale operations efficiently despite rapid growth. Enable proactive fraud prevention rather than reactive damage control, potentially saving $2-4M annually in disputed transaction losses.
