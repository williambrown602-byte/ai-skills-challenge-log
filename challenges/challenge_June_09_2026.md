As a senior data analyst at a growing management consulting firm, you've been tasked with solving a critical business intelligence problem. The firm's 45 active client engagements are generating inconsistent profit margins (ranging from -12% to +68%), and partners lack visibility into which client characteristics and engagement factors drive profitability. With $18M in annual revenue at stake, leadership needs a comprehensive client portfolio analysis system to identify optimization opportunities and inform strategic account management decisions for the upcoming fiscal year planning cycle.

🎯 Objective
Build an intelligent client portfolio optimization system that analyzes engagement profitability patterns, identifies high-value client characteristics, and generates strategic account management recommendations to improve overall portfolio margins by 15-25% within 12 months.

🧩 Technical Requirement
Develop a multi-dimensional analytical engine that processes client engagement data, performs statistical correlation analysis, segments clients by profitability drivers, and generates predictive insights for strategic portfolio optimization.

TOOL FOCUS FOR TODAY:
