DAILY PROFESSIONAL AI CHALLENGE – April 11, 2026

🏢 Business Scenario
You're the data strategy consultant for a mid-size logistics company experiencing delivery performance issues. The company handles 2,500+ daily deliveries across 3 regional hubs but lacks visibility into driver performance patterns, route inefficiencies, and customer satisfaction drivers. Management needs actionable insights to improve their 78% on-time delivery rate to industry standard of 92% while reducing fuel costs by 20%.

🎯 Objective
Build an intelligent delivery performance analytics system that identifies key performance drivers, predicts delivery risks, and generates optimization recommendations for route planning and driver management.

🧩 Technical Requirement
Create a comprehensive analytics engine that processes multi-dimensional logistics data to generate predictive models for delivery performance and automated recommendations for operational improvements.

TOOL FOCUS FOR TODAY:
Primarily Claude Code

📂 Inputs
- Historical delivery data (6 months): routes, times, delays, fuel consumption
- Driver performance metrics: experience levels, vehicle assignments, overtime patterns  
- Customer feedback data: delivery ratings, complaint categories, satisfaction scores
- Geographic data: delivery zones, traffic patterns, distance matrices
- Vehicle maintenance records and fuel efficiency data

📈 Business Deliverables
- Driver performance ranking system with improvement recommendations
- Route optimization suggestions showing potential time/fuel savings
- Delivery risk prediction model for next-day planning
- Customer satisfaction correlation analysis with operational factors
- Executive dashboard with KPIs and strategic recommendations

🧠 Business Impact
Transform logistics operations from reactive to predictive, enabling 15-20% cost reduction through optimized routes and improved driver productivity. Provide data-driven foundation for driver coaching, route planning, and customer service improvements that directly impact bottom line and competitive positioning.

- CSV/JSON data processing and manipulation
- Statistical analysis and predictive modeling libraries
- Data visualization and reporting tools
