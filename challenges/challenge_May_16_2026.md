DAILY PROFESSIONAL AI CHALLENGE – May 16, 2026

🏢 Business Scenario
You're the lead data analyst at a mid-sized regional bank facing increasing pressure from regulators and board members due to rising loan default rates (currently 4.2%, up from 2.1% last year). The Chief Risk Officer needs a comprehensive analysis system to identify early warning signals, assess portfolio concentration risks, and generate actionable recommendations for the monthly risk committee meeting. Manual risk assessment currently takes the team 3-4 days per month and often misses subtle pattern changes that could predict defaults 45-60 days in advance.

🎯 Objective
Build an intelligent loan portfolio risk monitoring system that predicts default probability, identifies high-risk segments, and generates strategic risk mitigation recommendations to reduce default rates back to industry standard (2.5%) within 12 months.

🧩 Technical Requirement
Create a comprehensive risk analysis engine that processes loan performance data, borrower characteristics, and economic indicators to generate predictive risk scores and strategic recommendations.

TOOL FOCUS FOR TODAY:
Primarily Claude Code

📂 Inputs
- Historical loan performance data (24 months, 8,500 loans)
- Current active loan portfolio (3,200 loans)
- Borrower demographic and financial profiles
- Regional economic indicators
- Industry risk benchmarks

📈 Business Deliverables
- Top 100 highest-risk active loans requiring immediate committee review
- Portfolio concentration risk analysis by geography, industry, and loan type
- Predictive default model with 60-day early warning system
- Strategic risk mitigation plan with projected impact on default rates
- Executive risk dashboard for monthly committee meetings

🧠 Business Impact
Transform reactive risk management into predictive oversight, potentially saving $2-4M annually in default losses while ensuring regulatory compliance and enabling confident lending growth. This system becomes the foundation for data-driven risk decisions and demonstrates advanced analytics capabilities to regulators.

🛠 Allowed Tools
- Claude Code (primary analysis environment)
- Python libraries (pandas, numpy, scikit-learn, matplotlib)
- Statistical modeling tools
- Data visualization capabilities

Target: 90-120 minutes

📁 Portfolio Artefact
Complete risk monitoring system with predictive models, executive dashboard, and strategic recommendations that demonstrates advanced financial risk analytics capabilities for banking/fintech roles.

- Executive-level risk communication
