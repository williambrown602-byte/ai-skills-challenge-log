🏢 Business Scenario
You're supporting the COO of a fast-growing B2B software company that's experiencing growing pains with vendor management. The company works with 180+ vendors across different categories (software, services, hardware, facilities), but lacks visibility into contract terms, renewal dates, and performance metrics. Recent incidents include a $45K surprise renewal, two service outages from underperforming vendors, and missed opportunities for volume discounts. The COO needs a comprehensive vendor intelligence system to transform reactive vendor management into strategic supplier optimization.

🎯 Objective
Create an intelligent vendor relationship analytics system that consolidates vendor data, identifies optimization opportunities, and generates strategic recommendations for improving vendor portfolio performance while reducing costs and risks.

🧩 Technical Requirement
Build a multi-dimensional vendor analysis engine that processes contract data, performance metrics, and spend patterns to generate vendor scorecards and strategic sourcing recommendations.

TOOL FOCUS FOR TODAY:
