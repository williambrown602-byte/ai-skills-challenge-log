Energy production has been underperforming industry benchmarks by 15-20%, costing approximately $2.3M annually in lost revenue. The operations team suspects equipment degradation and suboptimal maintenance schedules, but lacks data-driven insights to optimize performance. With Q3 investor meetings approaching, leadership needs a comprehensive asset performance strategy.

🎯 Objective
Transform 18 months of scattered solar farm performance data into an intelligent energy asset optimization system that identifies underperforming installations, predicts optimal maintenance windows, and generates strategic recommendations to restore energy output to industry-leading levels within 6 months.

🧩 Technical Requirement
Build a multi-dimensional performance analysis engine that processes weather data, energy output metrics, maintenance records, and equipment specifications to generate predictive insights and optimization recommendations using statistical modeling and pattern recognition algorithms.

TOOL FOCUS FOR TODAY:
Primarily Claude Code
