DAILY PROFESSIONAL AI CHALLENGE – May 26, 2026

🏢 Business Scenario
A mid-sized cybersecurity consulting firm is struggling with inconsistent project delivery quality and client satisfaction scores that have dropped to 72%. With 85+ active engagements across penetration testing, compliance audits, and security architecture, the firm lacks visibility into which project approaches drive success versus failure. Partners need data-driven insights to standardize delivery excellence and protect their reputation in a competitive market where one poor engagement can damage relationships worth $2M+ annually.

🎯 Objective
Transform 24 months of scattered engagement data into an intelligent project delivery optimization system that identifies success patterns, predicts project risks, and generates actionable quality assurance recommendations to restore client satisfaction above 90% within 6 months.

🧩 Technical Requirement
Build a multi-dimensional project analysis engine that processes engagement data, team compositions, and client outcomes to identify statistical patterns driving project success and generate predictive quality scores.

TOOL FOCUS FOR TODAY:

- Executive dashboard showing quality metrics and projected satisfaction recovery timeline

Protect firm reputation and revenue by preventing satisfaction issues before they damage client relationships. Create competitive advantage through consistent delivery excellence.
