DAILY PROFESSIONAL AI CHALLENGE – May 01, 2026

🏢 Business Scenario
You're a data analyst at a mid-sized architecture firm struggling with project delivery consistency. The firm has 180+ active projects across residential, commercial, and institutional sectors. Partners are concerned about frequent scope changes, budget overruns averaging 23%, and client satisfaction declining from 89% to 76%. The firm needs to transform project oversight from reactive firefighting into predictive project health management to protect margins and client relationships.

🎯 Objective
Build an intelligent project health monitoring system that analyzes project communications, budget tracking, and milestone data to predict project risks 3-4 weeks before they become critical issues, enabling proactive intervention and improved delivery outcomes.

🧩 Technical Requirement
Create a comprehensive project analytics engine that processes multi-source project data, identifies risk patterns through statistical analysis, and generates actionable early warning alerts with specific intervention recommendations.

TOOL FOCUS FOR TODAY:
Primarily Claude Code

📂 Inputs
- Project timeline and milestone data (180 projects over 18 months)
- Budget vs actual spending by project phase
- Client communication logs and change request history  
- Resource allocation and architect utilization data
- Historical project outcome data with success/failure indicators

📈 Business Deliverables
- Top 25 highest-risk projects requiring immediate partner attention with specific risk factors
- Predictive project health scoring system (0-100 scale) with trend analysis
- Early warning alert system for budget overrun and timeline slip risks
- Client relationship risk assessment based on communication patterns
- Weekly project portfolio health report for senior leadership

🧠 Business Impact
Transform reactive project management into predictive oversight, potentially reducing project overruns by 40% and improving client retention through proactive communication. Enable partners to focus intervention efforts on highest-impact opportunities while protecting firm reputation and margins.

🛠 Allowed Tools
Claude Code (primary), basic spreadsheet integration for data import/export

⏱ Time Box
Target: 90-120 minutes

📁 Portfolio Artefact
Project risk prediction system with real-time scoring algorithm and partner dashboard showcasing advanced business analytics and risk management capabilities

- Early warning system design and implementation
- Project management analytics and optimization
- Executive-level risk communication and reporting
