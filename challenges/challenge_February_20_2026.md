DAILY PROFESSIONAL AI CHALLENGE – February 20, 2026

🏢 Business Scenario
You're working as a business analyst at a fast-growing SaaS company that's struggling with customer onboarding efficiency. The customer success team reports that 35% of new customers don't complete onboarding within 30 days, leading to higher churn rates and delayed time-to-value. The VP of Customer Success needs a comprehensive analysis of the onboarding funnel to identify bottlenecks and create an optimization strategy before the board meeting next week.

🎯 Objective
Build an intelligent customer onboarding analytics system that identifies drop-off patterns, predicts completion likelihood, and generates actionable optimization recommendations to improve onboarding completion rates from 65% to 85%.

🧩 Technical Requirement
Create a multi-stage funnel analysis system with predictive modeling capabilities that processes customer journey data and generates automated insights with statistical validation.

TOOL FOCUS FOR TODAY:
Mixed Tools (Claude Code + Integration)

📂 Inputs
- Customer onboarding step completion data (8 key milestones)
- User demographic and firmographic data
- Support ticket data during onboarding period
- Product usage metrics for first 30 days
- Customer success manager interaction logs
- Industry and company size classifications

📈 Business Deliverables
- Onboarding funnel analysis with drop-off rates by step and customer segment
- Customer completion probability model for active onboarding cohorts
- Top 5 optimization recommendations with projected impact on completion rates
- Risk-based customer intervention priority list for customer success team
- Executive dashboard showing current vs. projected performance metrics

🧠 Business Impact
Improving onboarding completion from 65% to 85% could increase customer lifetime value by $2.1M annually, reduce customer success team reactive work by 40%, and improve net revenue retention. This creates measurable ROI through reduced churn and faster customer time-to-value.

🛠 Allowed Tools
- Claude Code for analysis and modeling
- Spreadsheet tool for data manipulation
- Database system (SQLite/Supabase) for data storage
- Make.com or similar for workflow automation
- Simple visualization tool for dashboard creation

⏱ Time Box
Target: 90-120 minutes

📁 Portfolio Artefact
Customer onboarding optimization system with predictive models, executive dashboard, and implementation roadmap demonstrating customer success analytics expertise.

🎓 Skills Trained
- Customer journey analytics and funnel optimization
- Predictive modeling for customer behavior
- Customer success metrics and KPI development
- Data-driven process improvement methodology
- SaaS business model understanding

🧪 Success Criteria
1. Onboarding funnel shows completion rates for each step with statistical significance
2. Predictive model achieves 80%+ accuracy in identifying at-risk customers
3. Optimization recommendations include specific actions with projected impact
4. Dashboard updates automatically with new customer data
5. Intervention priority list ranks customers by risk level and revenue potential

🚀 Stretch Goals

- Create customer success manager workload optimization based on intervention priorities
