🏢 Business Scenario
You're the operations analyst at a mid-sized insurance company experiencing significant inefficiencies in their claims processing workflow. The company processes 2,800+ claims monthly across auto, home, and commercial lines, but current manual routing and prioritization is causing 18-day average processing times and customer satisfaction scores of only 68%. The VP of Operations needs a comprehensive system to transform claims operations into a data-driven, efficient process that can achieve industry-leading performance metrics.

🎯 Objective
Build an intelligent claims processing optimization system that automates claim intake analysis, predicts processing complexity, optimizes adjuster workload distribution, and generates real-time performance insights to reduce processing time to under 10 days while improving customer satisfaction above 85%.

🧩 Technical Requirement
Develop a multi-algorithm system that processes claims data, performs risk stratification, implements dynamic workload balancing, and creates predictive models for processing time estimation with confidence intervals.

TOOL FOCUS FOR TODAY:

- Customer communication automation templates for different processing stages
- ROI projection showing potential cost savings and satisfaction improvements

🧠 Business Impact
