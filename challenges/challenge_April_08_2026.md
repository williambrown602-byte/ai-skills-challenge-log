DAILY PROFESSIONAL AI CHALLENGE – April 08, 2026

🏢 Business Scenario
You're working with a mid-sized manufacturing company that's struggling with workforce management across multiple shifts and production lines. The company has 180 employees across 3 shifts, with frequent absenteeism, overtime costs spiraling to $45K monthly, and production targets being missed 40% of the time due to understaffing. The HR and Operations teams currently spend 8 hours weekly manually juggling schedules, calling in replacements, and analyzing staffing patterns. Management needs a data-driven solution to optimize shift coverage while controlling labor costs.

🎯 Objective
Build an intelligent workforce scheduling optimization system that predicts staffing needs, identifies coverage gaps, and automatically generates shift recommendations to reduce overtime costs by 30% while maintaining 95%+ production target achievement.

🧩 Technical Requirement
Create a comprehensive scheduling analysis engine that processes historical attendance data, identifies patterns, and generates optimal staffing recommendations with predictive modeling for future scheduling needs.

TOOL FOCUS FOR TODAY:
Primarily Claude Code

📂 Inputs
- 18 months of employee attendance records (dates, shifts, departments, attendance status, overtime hours)
- Production line requirements (minimum staff per shift, skill requirements, productivity targets)
- Employee profiles (skills, availability preferences, performance ratings, hire dates)
- Historical production output data correlated with staffing levels
- Current overtime costs and absenteeism patterns by department and shift

📈 Business Deliverables
- Optimal shift staffing recommendations for next 4 weeks with coverage gap alerts
- Absenteeism prediction model identifying high-risk shifts requiring backup planning
- Overtime cost reduction strategy with specific implementation steps and projected savings
- Employee utilization analysis showing optimal skill-shift matches
- Weekly workforce planning dashboard with real-time staffing health indicators

🧠 Business Impact
Demonstrates ability to solve complex operational challenges that directly impact bottom-line profitability. Shows expertise in workforce analytics, a critical skill for operations roles. Produces measurable ROI through cost reduction and efficiency gains. Creates executive-ready insights for strategic workforce planning decisions.

🛠 Allowed Tools
- Claude Code (primary analysis and modeling)
- Excel/Google Sheets (data visualization if needed)
- Basic charting tools for executive presentation

⏱ Time Box
Target: 90-120 minutes

📁 Portfolio Artefact
Save the complete workforce optimization system including staffing recommendations, cost analysis, and executive dashboard. Document the methodology and projected business impact for interview discussions.

🎓 Skills Trained
- Workforce analytics and operational optimization
- Predictive modeling for business operations
- Multi-constraint scheduling and resource allocation
- Manufacturing industry operational knowledge
- Cost reduction strategy development and ROI calculation

1. Identifies at least 15 specific staffing optimization opportunities with quantified impact
2. Generates 4-week staffing recommendations with 85%+ coverage confidence
3. Produces overtime reduction strategy projecting 25-35% cost savings
4. Creates absenteeism prediction model with risk scoring for proactive planning
5. Delivers executive summary with top 3 implementation priorities and timeline
