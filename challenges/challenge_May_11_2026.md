DAILY PROFESSIONAL AI CHALLENGE – May 11, 2026

🏢 Business Scenario
You're the chief people analytics officer at a fast-growing consulting firm that has expanded from 85 to 280 employees in 18 months. The rapid growth has created significant compensation inconsistencies, with some roles showing 35-40% pay variance for equivalent work. Partners are concerned about retention risks, budget control, and potential equity issues. The firm needs a comprehensive compensation analysis to support upcoming annual reviews and ensure competitive positioning while controlling costs.

🎯 Objective
Transform scattered compensation data into an intelligent pay equity and market positioning system that identifies compensation gaps, ensures internal equity, and generates strategic salary adjustment recommendations with budget impact projections.

🧩 Technical Requirement
Build a multi-dimensional compensation analysis engine that processes salary data, role classifications, performance metrics, and market benchmarks to generate equity-adjusted pay recommendations with statistical confidence intervals.

TOOL FOCUS FOR TODAY:
Primarily Claude Code

📂 Inputs
- Employee compensation database (280+ employees across 15 roles)
- Performance review scores and promotion history
- Market salary survey data by role and geography
- Department budgets and headcount plans
- Retention risk indicators and exit interview data

📈 Business Deliverables
- Compensation equity analysis report identifying pay gaps by role, department, and demographic factors
- Market competitiveness assessment showing position against industry benchmarks
- Strategic salary adjustment recommendations with budget requirements
- Retention risk mitigation plan for underpaid high performers
- Partner presentation with implementation roadmap and projected ROI

🧠 Business Impact
Proactively address compensation inequities before they become retention or legal issues, ensure competitive market positioning to support growth hiring, optimize compensation budget allocation for maximum retention impact, and demonstrate data-driven people strategy leadership to partners and employees.

🛠 Allowed Tools
Claude Code (primary), basic data visualization, statistical analysis libraries, executive presentation templates

⏱ Time Box
Target: 90-120 minutes

📁 Portfolio Artefact
Comprehensive compensation analysis system with equity assessment, market positioning dashboard, and strategic adjustment recommendations - showcases advanced people analytics and executive decision support capabilities.

🎓 Skills Trained
- Advanced people analytics and compensation modeling
- Multi-factor equity analysis with statistical significance testing
- Strategic HR decision support and budget impact analysis
- Executive-level business communication for sensitive HR topics
- Predictive modeling for retention risk assessment

🧪 Success Criteria
1. Identify all compensation gaps >15% for equivalent roles with statistical confidence
2. Generate market competitiveness scores for each role with benchmark data
3. Produce budget-optimized salary adjustment plan staying within 8% annual increase budget
4. Create retention risk priority list showing top 50 flight-risk employees
5. Develop implementation timeline with projected retention improvement metrics

🚀 Stretch Goals
- Build predictive promotion timeline models showing career progression equity
- Create real-time compensation monitoring dashboard for ongoing equity tracking
- Develop automated salary offer calculator for new hire negotiations
- Generate diversity and inclusion impact analysis of current compensation patterns
