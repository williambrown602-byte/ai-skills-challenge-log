DAILY PROFESSIONAL AI CHALLENGE – April 30, 2026

🏢 Business Scenario
You work for a mid-sized technology company that recently acquired three smaller firms over the past 18 months. The integration process has been chaotic, with duplicate systems, inconsistent data formats, and overlapping vendor contracts across all four entities. The CFO needs to present a comprehensive post-acquisition optimization plan to the board next week, but currently lacks visibility into the true scope of redundancies and integration opportunities.

🎯 Objective
Build an intelligent post-merger integration analytics system that identifies redundancies, quantifies consolidation opportunities, and generates a strategic optimization roadmap with projected cost savings and implementation timelines.

🧩 Technical Requirement
Create a multi-dimensional analysis engine that processes organizational data across systems, contracts, personnel, and operations to identify consolidation opportunities and calculate financial impact scenarios.

TOOL FOCUS FOR TODAY:
Primarily Claude Code

📂 Inputs
- Employee roster data from all 4 companies (roles, salaries, departments, locations)
- Software license inventory showing 180+ applications across entities
- Vendor contract database with 240+ active agreements
- Office lease information for 12 locations
- Department budget data for past 24 months
- Organizational charts showing reporting structures

📈 Business Deliverables
- Comprehensive redundancy analysis report with quantified savings opportunities
- Strategic integration roadmap with 90-day, 6-month, and 12-month phases
- Risk-adjusted cost savings projections with confidence intervals
- Vendor consolidation recommendations prioritized by impact and complexity
- Real estate optimization plan with relocation/closure recommendations

🧠 Business Impact
Enable data-driven M&A integration decisions that could save $2-4M annually while reducing operational complexity. Demonstrate analytical capabilities crucial for corporate development roles, strategy consulting, and senior finance positions in growth companies.

🛠 Allowed Tools
- Claude Code (primary)
- Spreadsheet applications for data visualization
- Basic presentation software for executive summary

⏱ Time Box
Target: 90-120 minutes

📁 Portfolio Artefact
Save the complete integration analysis system, executive dashboard, and strategic recommendations as a showcase of M&A analytical capabilities for strategy and finance roles.

🎓 Skills Trained
- Post-merger integration analytics and optimization
- Multi-entity organizational analysis and strategic planning
- Executive-level financial modeling with scenario planning
- Corporate development business intelligence
- Strategic cost reduction and operational efficiency analysis

🧪 Success Criteria
1. System identifies at least 15 specific consolidation opportunities with dollar impact
2. Integration roadmap includes realistic timelines and implementation complexity scores
3. Cost savings projections include both optimistic and conservative scenarios
4. Analysis covers all major operational areas (personnel, systems, facilities, vendors)
5. Executive summary is board-ready with clear recommendations and financial justification

🚀 Stretch Goals
- Include employee retention risk analysis for key personnel during integration
- Add competitive positioning analysis showing how consolidation improves market strength
- Create automated monitoring system to track integration progress against plan
