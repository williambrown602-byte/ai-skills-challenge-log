DAILY PROFESSIONAL AI CHALLENGE – May 13, 2026

🏢 Business Scenario
You're a data analyst at a mid-sized telecommunications company experiencing significant network performance issues. Customer complaints about dropped calls and slow data speeds have increased 40% over the past quarter, while network infrastructure costs have grown by $2.3M. The CTO needs immediate insights to optimize network performance, reduce operational costs, and improve customer satisfaction before the quarterly board meeting in 10 days.

🎯 Objective
Build an intelligent network performance optimization system that analyzes multi-tower performance data, identifies service quality bottlenecks, predicts failure risks, and generates actionable infrastructure investment recommendations to restore customer satisfaction above 85% while reducing monthly network costs by 15-20%.

🧩 Technical Requirement
Develop a comprehensive network analytics engine that processes tower performance metrics, correlates service quality with customer complaint patterns, and creates predictive models for proactive network maintenance and resource allocation optimization.

TOOL FOCUS FOR TODAY:
Primarily Claude Code

📂 Inputs
- 18 months of tower performance data (uptime, capacity utilization, signal strength)
- Customer complaint logs with geographic mapping and service type categorization
- Network infrastructure cost breakdowns by tower and maintenance category
- Historical maintenance schedules and outage incident reports
- Customer satisfaction scores by service area and time period

📈 Business Deliverables
- Tower performance ranking system identifying top 50 underperforming assets
- Network optimization recommendations with projected customer satisfaction improvement
- Predictive maintenance calendar showing optimal service windows for next 6 months
- Infrastructure investment priority matrix with ROI projections
- Executive dashboard with real-time network health indicators

🧠 Business Impact
This system transforms reactive network management into predictive optimization, potentially saving $3-5M annually in reduced downtime costs while improving customer retention through better service quality. The insights enable data-driven infrastructure decisions and proactive maintenance scheduling.

🛠 Allowed Tools

- Spreadsheet tools for data organization
- Basic visualization capabilities
- Statistical analysis functions
